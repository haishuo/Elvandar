**Notes:** This chapter is where the expedition's working names for Lathion's locations originate. Not Lathionese proper names — those died with the civilization. The scholars coin practical labels out of necessity: the uppermost level becomes "the Crown" (it sits at the top), the vast chamber of brass objects becomes "the Repository," the threshold space at the conveyor's base gets called "the Gatehouse," etc. The reader gets the terminology the same way the characters do: improvised, human, stuck by use.

Elara begins reading fragments here — not fluently, not explainably, but usefully. Xion and Voss begin their parallel practical track alongside the scholarly one. The wonder of Chapter 5 has become context rather than shock, which is its own kind of extraordinary.

**Sets up:** Chapter 7 (The Repository) — deeper exploration, more specific discoveries.
