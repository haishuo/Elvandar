# Nessa Halcin: The Other Half of the Orb Weaver

## The Handmaid

Nessa Halcin was placed in the Imperial Palace as Princess Melina's personal handmaid when both girls were
small, and she was placed there for a reason. House Halcin is a minor house — a name, a charter and not much
else — and a daughter in the princess's own rooms was the best piece of access the family had ever been
offered. Her parents saw a ladder. That is all she was meant to be.

It did not stay that. Somewhere between the ages at which neither girl can remember a first meeting, the
appointment turned into the friendship it was supposed to look like. They grew up in the same rooms, learned
the same court on the same days, and became what Kai Issamil is to Tiberian: **almost a sister**, and on the
private side of the princess's composure. They are thick as thieves. They giggle about boys at sleepovers.
Nessa is one of the two people alive — Tiberian is the other — who knows that the cool, unreadable Princess
Melina feels a great deal and is simply not expressive.

And it was Nessa who said, at the start of Book 6, *we could just ask them. Not as you.*

## Paradigm Analysis

**Caretaker → Spirited Competitor → Founder → Merchant → Truth Seeker → Engineer → Creator**

*(Proposed 2026-11-01; approved by the author 2026-11-02.)*

**Her stack is drawn to complement Melina's rather than to resemble it**, on the same geometry the series
already uses twice: Xion and Kael, then Tiberian and Kai. Melina runs Truth Seeker → Engineer at the top and
Merchant at the very bottom. Nessa runs Caretaker → Spirited Competitor, with **Merchant in the middle of her
stack where Melina has none at all.** That is not decoration. It is the answer to how a Merchant-last princess
runs a profitable underworld brokerage: **she doesn't. Nessa does the commerce.** Melina supplies the engine
that turns a thousand fragments into a picture; Nessa knows what a fragment is worth and to whom.

### Caretaker (Primary)

Aimed at one person, and the person is Melina. **This is not Farleen's shape and must not be written as
it.** Farleen has one large intrinsic drive and a set of trained instruments; strip out the Caretaker and the
operative has nothing to operate on. Nessa has a real second drive, and she would have it with or without
Melina. What the Caretaker primary settles is only the order of surrender: when the friend and the game
collide, the friend wins.

### Spirited Competitor (Secondary)

**She enjoys it.** The lie, the cut-outs, the customer who thinks he is dealing with a frightened go-between
three removes from the real broker — that is play to her, and the Orb Weaver is the best game anyone in the
palace has ever been offered. This is the drive the brokerage feeds, and it is the reason the brokerage
**means something to her that it does not mean to Melina.** See *What it costs her*, below.

### Founder (Third)

The Orb Weaver was her idea. She built the first of it — the first contact, the first cut-out, the first
price — before Melina had decided it was sound. **Do not let it become retroactively Melina's.** The
princess made it the best broker in Kaha'an; the handmaid made it exist.

### Merchant (Fourth)

Learned at home. A minor house lives one bad season from losing its name, and a girl raised in one knows
moneylenders, debt-writs and the price of a favour in a way no princess can. **This is where the doorway into
the underworld comes from**: not from Melina's genius, but from the kind of family that is never far from it.

### Truth Seeker, Engineer, Creator (the rest)

Low, and that is the partnership working. Melina is the analyst; Nessa does not need to be one. She is not
stupid and must never be written as the slower girl. She is the quicker one in a room full of people, and the
slower one beside a mind nobody can follow, which is a different thing.

## The Orb Weaver

**An underworld information broker in Kaha'an, run jointly by Melina and Nessa, known to nobody else.**
Full account in `People/Melina Valanar.md`, *The Orb Weaver*; Book 6 placement in `book6_outline.md`.
What belongs on this sheet is Nessa's half of it.

**She is the face that is not a face.** The Orb Weaver deals through intermediaries, and Nessa is the last of
them that anyone can reach. If she is ever asked, she says that she too is a go-between, that she passes notes
to someone who passes them to someone else, and that she has never met the Orb Weaver either. **She is lying
through her teeth, and she is very good at it.** That lie is what keeps the princess three removes from
anyone who might work it out — and it is the most exposed position in the arrangement. The Orb Weaver can be
burned. **Nessa is what would be standing in the fire.**

### The cover has a second floor *(author's design, 2026-11-01)*

**The obvious leak is that she lives in the palace.** Sooner or later someone in the trade puts it together:
*the go-between is the princess's own handmaid — maybe she* is *the Orb Weaver.* **Nessa does not deny the
connection. She confesses to a smaller one.** Of course she is an informant for the Orb Weaver. A broker of
that calibre would naturally have informants everywhere, including the imperial palace.

**It is a confession built to be believed, and it does three things at once.** It explains why she is close to
the source, by admitting she *is* a source. It explains why the Orb Weaver ever knows anything from inside the
palace. And **it advertises**: a broker with a source in the princess's own rooms is worth more to
every customer who hears it. The fallback story sells the business while it protects it.

**Press her on how much time she spends with the princess and the answer is the job itself.** *Of course she
does. She is Her Imperial Highness's personal handmaid; it would be quite difficult to do the work from three
rooms away.* **This is the Spirited Competitor at play**, and it should read like it: she is enjoying the
question.

**The two floors are for different audiences, and that limits them.** *Informant* is a cover for the
underworld, where a palace informant is natural and valuable. **It is not a cover inside the palace**, where
an admitted informant is a security matter and the end of her post. To the palace she is only ever a
handmaid. **Never write a scene where she plays the underworld story to a palace audience**; it would be the
mistake of someone much less good at this than she is.

**It is tested once and it holds.** Between Book 7 and Book 8 she works the waverers of a conspiracy against
Tiberian, as the broker's face, and the informant cover comes through unburned. See `Offstage.md`, *The
Children of the Great Hunt*.

### What it costs her

**Melina has no emotional attachment to the Orb Weaver; it is a tool, and she would burn it to protect her
family.** That is settled on her sheet. **It is not true of Nessa.** The brokerage is her idea, her doorway,
her game and her Spirited Competitor's whole playground, and she has made it into something. If it ever has to
be burned, she will agree — Caretaker first — and she will mind, and **the minding is hers to carry, not
Melina's.**

**There is no planned burning.** *(Author's ruling, 2026-11-01: no reason to burn the Orb Weaver unless the
logic of the story calls for it.)* This section exists so that, if the logic ever does call for it, the cost
lands on the right person.

## Guardrails

**1. The Orb Weaver was her idea.** Never retell its origin with Melina proposing it.

**2. She is not an exposition device for Melina's interior.** She knows what is under the mask; she does not
explain it to anyone, least of all to the reader. Melina's sheet forbids anyone from naming the softest place
in her, and that applies to Nessa as much as to anyone. What Nessa knows shows in what she does — the cushion
she has already fetched, the question she does not ask — never in what she says about her friend.

**3. The friendship is warm and it is light, and that lightness is load-bearing.** Book 6 is the greyest book
of the eight. The giggling over boys is real and stays on the page. **It is platonic.** Do not turn it into a
romance subplot or a source of jealousy unless the author places one.

**4. Farleen does not know.** *(Author's ruling, 2026-11-01.)* Nobody does except the two of them. **Her own
family does not know either** — they placed her for influence and have no idea what she has built with it.
That is background and nothing more: no scene depends on it, and it is not a seed. If a story ever needs a way
for the secret to leak, it is there; until one does, leave it alone.

**5. She is a noblewoman and she carries a house name.** *Halcin* is a native charter-name on a minor house —
see `Names.md` for the coinage. Her given name was kept on the author's ruling, 2026-11-02.

## Relationships

### Melina Valanar

Almost a sister. See above, and Melina's sheet. **The partnership is the Kai–Tiberian geometry on the
princess's side:** complementary stacks, a childhood in the same rooms, and one of the pair supplying exactly
what the other lacks. Tiberian charges and Kai makes sure there is ground to come back to; Melina sees the whole
web and Nessa knows what each thread will sell for.

### Tiberian Valanar

Has known her all his life, as everyone in the princess's household has. **He does not know about the Orb
Weaver.** That costs Melina, not Nessa — see Melina's sheet.

### Her family

House Halcin wanted a ladder and got one. They do not know where it goes.
