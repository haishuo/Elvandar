# Irene Umberlow — The Last Paper Lord

## Paradigm stack

**Merchant → Caretaker.** The remaining five ranks and the percentages are unsettled.

Irene enjoys making money. The satisfaction is in seeing a sound deal through and having more to
work with afterward. The Caretaker in her knows what that money buys the people she loves. She grew
up in a house that could scarcely absorb another bad month and wants them to live without the fear
of having to sell something they need. She will still take pleasure in the next deal when the reserve
is deep enough to see them through.

## Function

Irene is the baroness of an impoverished house. The Rending destroyed the Paper Lords' banking
fortune, and the family's knowledge of credit, collateral and payment survived it. Irene knows
that an impressive book of debts is only as sound as the people and goods behind it. She is trying
to build a business that can pay its obligations and put something aside.

Irene's intelligence shows up in the terms of a bargain. She knows who needs to be paid by nightfall,
what it costs to hold the cargo until tomorrow and what she can offer besides a higher price.
Melina follows a problem until she can see how all its parts fit. Irene can recognize the one term
that gives both sides a reason to agree and move while the offer is still good. With Tiberian, the
difference might be as ordinary as a shipment of shields. He can tell whether he would put one in
a soldier's hands. She would want to know what the supplier is charging for the whole order.

She meets Tiberian while bargaining on Vessel Street. She likes him, but for much of the courtship
she expects him to lose interest and return to the daughters of richer houses. She is angry enough
to speak plainly when he intervenes in her business. What changes her mind about him is that he
comes back, has taken her objection seriously and apologizes. By the time she agrees to marry him
she has made him promise that she will be the one to restore House Umberlow.

[The Marriage of the Tiger Prince](../The%20Marriage%20of%20the%20Tiger%20Prince.md) holds the first
meeting, the quarrel, the promise and the blue dress. [The house register](../Places/Kaha%27an/houses_of_kahaan.md#house-umberlow---the-paper-lords)
holds the history of the barony and the fall of its banking business.

## Guardrails

- Her financial expertise predates the courtship. Let her use it on work that would still matter
  to her if she had never met Tiberian.
- The restoration is hers to direct. The marriage gives House Umberlow no special claim to Crown
  subsidies, discreet debt payments or favored contracts.
- She can ask Tiberian for help and take pleasure in sharing a success with him. He leaves her
  the choice of what to ask for.
- Sending Celestine the blue dress is a small revenge she allows herself. Celestine's opinion
  takes up very little of Irene's working life.
