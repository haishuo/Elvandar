# Aurelia Alric — The Suitable Countess

## Paradigm stack

Unsettled.

## Function

Aurelia comes from the household of Count Alric. She is accomplished, easy in court society and
familiar with what her family expects of her there. The Alrics want a royal marriage more urgently
than she does. She thinks Tiberian handsome and kind and would be pleased to marry him. She can also
imagine a perfectly good life if he chooses someone else.

She belongs to Celestine's circle. She enjoys being welcome there, and it is easier to laugh when
Celestine makes a remark at Irene's expense than to risk embarrassing a friend in company. She seldom
begins these encounters herself. Her laughter still helps Celestine establish how the room will take
the remark. There is no established personal feud between Aurelia and Irene.

See [The Marriage of the Tiger Prince](../Offstage/The%20Marriage%20of%20the%20Tiger%20Prince.md) for the
Alrics' pursuit of the match and Aurelia's part in the blue-dress incident.

## Guardrails

- Give her the ordinary warmth and ease that make her good company. Her willingness to laugh along
  is a real weakness and should come at Irene's expense.
- Her family supplies most of the ambition behind the match. Aurelia can enjoy Tiberian's company
  without being in love with him.
- Her response to the marriage and her own future match remain open.
