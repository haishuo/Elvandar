# Basic Physical Characteristics — Canon Reference

This is the series-wide visual continuity ledger for principal and next-generation characters. It records
basic physical facts in one place so height, build, colouring, age, and distinguishing marks do not have to
be reconstructed from separate sheets or inferred from whichever piece of art happens to be open.

It is deliberately conservative. **Unspecified means unspecified.** An empty field is not permission to
copy a trait from old art, and an image-generation choice does not become prose canon merely because it
looks convincing.

## Authority and status

The codes in the tables identify where a trait comes from:

- **A — Author ruling:** explicitly settled by the author, including approved visual-design decisions
  copied into this ledger.
- **T — Textual canon:** explicit in chapter prose, a governing beat draft/outline, or an authoritative
  timeline.
- **S — Sheet:** stated on the individual character sheet but not independently fixed in prose.
- **U — Unset:** not established. Do not infer it from old art.

This ledger consolidates canon; it does not reverse the repository's normal authority rule. For Book 1,
finished prose outranks the retrofitted beat drafts, then the character sheet. For Books 2–8, the governing
beat draft or outline outranks derived prose, then the character sheet. **An explicit author ruling outranks
all three.** If two sources disagree, record the conflict here rather than silently choosing the more
convenient description.

Approved reference art may preserve an **A** decision that is not presently spoken on the page. Art by
itself is downstream evidence, not authority. Non-canon and alternate-universe images never establish a
trait.

## Height calibration standard

Exact heights are measured without heels, from a shared level floor to the crown of the head rather than
the hair. Record both feet/inches and centimeters. For children and adolescents, attach the measurement to
a book/age; do not silently carry a sixteen-year-old height into adulthood.

Technical calibration images use one fixed **armorer's fitting frame**: the same neutral stone wall, wooden
upright, brass interval bands, level floor, square camera position, lens, and subject distance every time.
The character stands alone, full-body, neutrally posed, in flat-soled footwear. These plates are measurement
references, not scenes and not identity portraits. The written number in this ledger remains authoritative
if image perspective, posture, hair, or footwear creates an apparent discrepancy.

**The first height set was fixed by authorial ruling on 2026-08-20:**

- Xion and Elara are each **5′11″ / 180 cm**. Neither is taller. **A**
- Farleen is **5′7″ / 170 cm**, four inches shorter than Xion and Elara. **A**
- Rosik is **6′1″ / 185 cm** in Book 1. **A**
- At sixteen, Tiberian is **5′10″ / 178 cm** and Melina Valanar is **5′8″ / 173 cm**. **A**
- Sa Ko Ren remains textually tall, but her exact height is not yet fixed. **T/U**

**Added by authorial ruling on 2026-08-21:**

- Kalden Erulius is **5′11″ / 180 cm** at the series opening. **A**
