# Book 9: Fair Game — High-Level Outline

**Status: seed.** *(Folder created 2026-11-21 on the author's direction.)* This file holds only what is
settled. The series outline and the Book 8 outline still describe eight books and have not yet been updated;
that is deferred, not forgotten. The title is a working title.

**Narrative Engine:** Uratha's civil war. After Book 8 the Council fails to remove Leowin Draskin, Kandor
Belus tries to arrest him with a small strike on the Ivory Tower, the strike fails, and Draskin declares him
in rebellion. The account up to that declaration is in
[The Failed Vote Against Draskin](../Offstage/The%20Failed%20Vote%20Against%20Draskin.md) and
[The Strike on the Ivory Tower](../Offstage/The%20Strike%20on%20the%20Ivory%20Tower.md); if the book stages the
strike, that account moves here.

**POV:** Mainly Kandor Belus (`People/Kandor Belus.md`), on the model of Book 4, which is told entirely from
Harkim's. Whether it is strictly single-POV or carries a secondary thread is open.

**Timeline:** Begins after Book 8, following the failed vote. Length not set. The saga measures in months.

**The title.** *Fair Game* carries three meanings, and nobody on the page states any of them: a fair contest,
which is what Belus believed he was owed; a legitimate target, which is what the declaration makes him; and a
game at all, because Draskin *enjoys politics*.
