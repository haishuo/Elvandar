# Pre-Rending Litrava

**Status:** Canonical historical geography, established 2026-08-22<br>
**Period:** The Province of Litrava within late-imperial and residual Balisha before the Rending<br>
**Language authority:** `Languages/Design Plates/Modern Balishan - Plate 2.md`

Pre-Rending Litrava was a country, not decorative farmland around a city-state. Kaha'an was its provincial
center, the capital of Balisha, and its largest port, but inland cities, agricultural markets, a northern
forest economy, irrigation works, road stations, and minor coastal trade all functioned independently of
the capital. Merchants could accept a longer journey through the northern roads to avoid Kaha'an's tariffs.

The Rending destroyed that network in hours. Kaha'an survived inside its wards. Outside them, cities,
forest, farms, river courses, milestones, and recognizable roads disappeared beneath a lethal transformed
landscape. The names below remain in archives and living memory, but almost none still describes a usable
destination.

## Geographic frame

- **South:** The reef-riddled **Maukaras** coast and the Azure Sea. Kaha'an's protected **Maulom** basin
  was the province's only deep-water harbor.
- **North:** **Dunyelas**, the great deciduous forest, and beyond it the southern foothills of the Sentinel
  Mountains.
- **Center:** **Limel Mar**, the Great Plain, much of which was cultivated as **Gonmel Mar**, the Great
  Grainlands.
- **West:** The river approaches through **Talrava**, connecting the province to inland trade.
- **East:** The canal country around **Tirun** and roads toward the Erulian frontier.

## Cities and towns

| Place | Role before the Rending | Fate |
|---|---|---|
| **Talrava** | Western river city, carrier center, and inland gate to Litrava | City and defining river course destroyed |
| **Gonmer** | Central grain exchange and storage market | Destroyed with the Grainlands |
| **Yelava** | Woodland city and western anchor of the northern bypass | Destroyed with Dunyelas |
| **Unava** | Aqueduct headworks city and water-distribution center | Destroyed; portions of the high and capital-facing aqueduct later repaired |
| **Tirun** | Eastern canal town, irrigation center, and road market | Destroyed; buried canal survives only as an archival line |
| **Feshber** | Shallow-draft salt and fishing landing west of Kaha'an | Destroyed; reefs remain |
| **Durcai** | Foothill crossing town and eastern anchor of the northern bypass | Balishan settlement destroyed; mountain crossing survives under other names |

## Landscape and infrastructure

| Place | Historical function | Present condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Dunyelas** | Northern deciduous forest and timber-wolf homeland | Ecology erased; old **Deepwood Forest** label retired |
| **Dunkargar** | Imperial northern fortress and site of the buried Valanar Vault | Surface fortress erased; vault survival unknown; old **Fort Dinkarth** label retired |
| **Limel Mar** | Great central plain | Buried beneath modern dunes |
| **Gonmel Mar** | Principal cultivated grain belt | Sterile transformed ground |
| **Yeltal** | Forest river feeding western irrigation | Lower course destroyed or diverted; buried bed called **Yeltal Mor** in survivor speech |
| **Yelpas** | Maintained forest-road network allowing caravans to bypass Kaha'an | No longer exists as a route |
| **Unpel Dun** | Northern imperial aqueduct from the mountain source through Unava | High and capital-facing portions survive or were rebuilt; lowland network severed |
| **Maulom** | Kaha'an's protected deep-water harbor basin | Survived inside the city's protected geography |
| **Maukaras** | Reef Coast surrounding Maulom | Physical reefs survive; settlements and working coast do not |

## The bypass that died

The northern alternative was a chain, not a single magic road. Caravans entered through Talrava, moved
through Gonmer or directly toward Yelava, crossed Dunyelas on the Yelpas roads, and emerged near Durcai
before rejoining the eastern network. The route took longer and required hired protection through the less
patrolled woodland stretches. It was nevertheless ordinary commercial geography.

The Rending killed Talrava, Gonmer, Yelava, Dunyelas, Yelpas, and Durcai together. Losing any one would
have inconvenienced the bypass; losing all of them made the concept meaningless. This is why Kaha'an became
the continent's unavoidable land chokepoint at the same moment it lost the country that had fed it.

## The fortress and the Vault

Dunkargar stood on the Great Plain immediately south of Dunyelas. It guarded the forest roads and northern
approaches, making it a credible place for an emperor to conceal a reserve treasury without building a
conspicuous structure devoted only to the vault.

The Rending erased the fortress above ground. The subterranean Valanar Vault may remain intact, but surviving
records locate it only relationally: beneath Dunkargar, south of Dunyelas, on a plain that no longer exists.
Modern dunes preserve none of those coordinates. An authentic old map can therefore be accurate and
completely useless.

## Naming restraint

These are historical names, not promised story vocabulary. The reader can understand the loss of Litrava
from “the old forest,” “the buried towns,” and “the northern bypass” whenever an untranslated endonym would add
friction without value. The names exist so that archives, maps, families, and future historical references
have a coherent country behind them—not so the narration can display the dictionary.
