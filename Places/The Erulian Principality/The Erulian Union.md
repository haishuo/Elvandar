# The Erulian Union: The Breadbasket That Left

**A republic of farmers and merchants, formed out of a third of the Erulian Principality after the Compact
War.** It took the best farmland on the continent, the largest port in Elvandar, and the institutions that ran
both, and it did so without a battle, because the prince it left had no army to send after it.

**It never appears on the page.** Erulius is absent from Book 8, and no outline visits the Union. This file
exists so that the references to it scattered across Kalden's sheet, `Offstage.md`, the Kaha'an economy and
the Book 5 and Book 7 outlines stay consistent, and so that the border is drawn once. *(Author's design,
2026-11-02.)*

---

## How it formed

**The order is the whole explanation, and it is already canon** (`People/Kalden Erulius.md`; `Offstage.md`,
*The Compact War*). Kalden lost the war. He met the reparations by taxing his people and kept the court in full
splendour, and he stopped paying the army. **The army walked away first**: the Provincial Legions, after more
than a decade of arrears, some four or five years before Book 5. Within a year a third of the principality
refused taxation and formed the Union, **so it is three or four years old when Book 5 opens**, and its trade
with Kaha'an is new. *(Author's ruling, 2026-11-02.)* The Aspirant Guard is a small sworn elite, not the
army, and it stayed with Kalden and thinned. **It was bloodless because there was nobody left to send.** It was
not a revolution. It was a repudiation, and the most damning judgment on Kalden's reign is that he was not
overthrown but *left*.

**The twelve houses of the High Council did not lead it.** They stayed in the rump, as the men who counselled
austerity and were overruled, and in Book 7 they are Kalden's creditors and end up owning what remains of his
state (`book7_outline.md`, *Who held the paper*). **The secession came from below them**: the provincial
freeholders the principality had always taxed hard, the farming communities, and the market towns.

## Territory

**The Union holds:**

- **The Heartlands**: the Breadbasket Plains, the Orchard Hills, and the river country of the Ennor, the
  Tethian and the Alder.
- **The Golden Valley**, the farmland around Eruliath. **The border runs close under the capital's walls**, so
  Kalden kept his city and lost the fields that fed it.
- **Verdant Plains**, which is its seat. It has the Grain Exchange, the Agricultural College and the Farmers'
  Assembly.
- **Port Valen**, with the largest harbour facilities in Elvandar, the Naval Academy, the Foreign Quarter and
  the Erulian Navy.
  **This one is forced.** The rump is *"a landlocked mining operation"* that needs Silaris's harbour, and it
  cannot also hold the biggest port on the continent.

**The rump keeps** Eruliath, the northern mines and Northcrest, and some of the western marches. **The mining
conscription system stays with the mines**, and the Union never had it.

**Unassigned:** the inland woods. Nothing depends on them.

---

## Government

**A republic.** The Farmers' Assembly at Verdant Plains, which already existed as the meeting hall of the
agricultural communities, is where it grew from. Nothing more about its constitution is set, and nothing
needs to be.

---

## Trade

### With Kaha'an: its first order of business

**Each side has exactly what the other lacks.** Kaha'an is a desert city that has imported nearly all its food
since the Rending. It is the only deep-water harbour in its region, the second port of the continent after
Port Valen, and arguably the largest and wealthiest commercial city in Elvandar, and it is richer still after
Hauren. **The Union grows grain and owns the biggest port there is.** The trade runs by sea from Port Valen,
because overland the rump's western marches lie between them.

**Kaha'an wants it for reasons of its own.** After the Grain cartel was broken, Elara and Xion set out to
feed the city **without ever again depending on a single exporter**, which is the condition that produced the
Grain in the first place. The Union is a second source, not a replacement for the first. **This is the same
reasoning that makes Balisha choose the Green Cities in Book 5**: never be strangled through one supplier
again.

**What the trade does not do.** It is a market. It sells to buyers who can pay, and **it does not reach the
Warrens**. It is not the empty Grain chair refilled, since nobody coordinates it, and it does not prevent Book
6's famine, where the market will not haul bulk grain down to customers with no money (Master List, *How
Kaha'an eats, and the empty chair*). **Never write it as a solution to the Warrens.** It is the reason the
Noble District eats well while the Warrens do not.

### With the rump: less than geography would predict

**The rump is the Union's natural partner**, being the closest and sharing a border, roads and rivers. **They
trade, but far below the volume you would expect.** Kalden would sooner die than borrow from the secessionists
(`book7_outline.md`), and the Union remembers being taxed to death to pay for his court. Neither side wants
the other, and both need it a little.

---

## Kalden

**He is livid, and he is certain Xion arranged it.** The provinces that betrayed him are, within months,
doing business with Kaha'an. **To Kalden that is proof**: *the snake's hand is in this.* It is false, and
nobody arranged anything. The Union went to Kaha'an because that is where the money and the hunger are.

**It is one of the first bricks in the overmind.** Kalden's sheet records that after the war the *nobody
healer* is promoted to a mastermind who arranges continents, because losing to a nobody is unbearable (*The
imaginary Xion*). **The Union trade is the kind of evidence that promotion feeds on**: real, visible, and
pointing in the direction he needs. Every step of the escalation stays his own. **Nobody in the books corrects
him, and the narration does not either.**
