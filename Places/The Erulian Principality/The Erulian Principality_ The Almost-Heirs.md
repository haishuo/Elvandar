# The Erulian Principality: The Almost-Heirs

## Origins and Transformation

Once merely a province governed by a distant relative of the emperor who held the largely ceremonial title of "prince," the Erulian Principality gained new significance after The Rending. With the imperial line of Balisha believed to be cut and the emperor and empress presumed dead, the ruling house of Erulius suddenly found itself as the closest surviving relatives to the throne—though still not close enough to legitimately claim it.

### The Vanity Title

The Principality's very name began as a way to end an argument. Its founder — the first Lord Erulius, a third cousin twice removed on the mother's side of the Valanar emperor of his age — was a man of no real consequence who could not stop reminding the court of his imperial blood. For years he petitioned for a commission befitting his supposed station, until the emperor, whose seat then stood at Vartonne, silenced him in the cheapest way available: a grand-sounding but empty title — *Prince* — and a strip of remote frontier land to go with it, about as far from the Vartonne court as the empire reached, on the eastern coast where Eruliath now stands. In substance the grant was a marquisate; in style it was a Principality, and the flattering discrepancy cost the crown nothing. For its first century the holding was exactly what it had been designed to be: a quiet border march whose lord could style himself a prince and trouble no one. Only much later — when Terinok's secession began the slow cascade that unmade the empire, and Erulius drifted loose along with the other provinces — did the vanity title come free of the throne that bestowed it. And only after the Rending, with every Valanar believed dead, did a title invented to shut a man up become the foundation of a claim to the world.

### From Provincial Outpost to Ambitious Power

For approximately 80 years after the empire's collapse, the Erulian Principality existed in relative obscurity. While maintaining theoretical claims to imperial connection, the pre-Rending principality was content to manage its abundant resources and maintain its autonomy, with only a simmering undercurrent of jealousy toward Kaha'an's more direct imperial lineage.

The Rending changed everything. Kalden's father had died after an illness only months earlier, leaving a fifteen-year-old prince whom the High Council confirmed without appointing a regent. He inherited a stable state, experienced ministers, and institutions capable of carrying the ordinary work while their young ruler learned. Then the catastrophic magical event that transformed Balisha's surroundings into desert presumably ended the imperial line. The newly ascended Kalden recognized a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. His father's stories had taught him the vague family lore that House Erulius was related to the Valanars; the Rending sent him into the archives to recover the exact chain. What had been dynastic trivia suddenly became viable—if not to claim the Ruby Throne directly, then at least to position Erulius as the most legitimate heir to imperial tradition.

Prince Kalden transformed this opportunity into an organizing principle for his realm. The previously modest provincial capital rapidly expanded, imperial traditions were revived and adapted, and Erulian diplomatic efforts became increasingly focused on undermining Balisha's claims while building a coalition of supporters. The principality's abundant resources, previously dedicated to comfortable self-sufficiency, were redirected toward this greater ambition.

This transformation was not universally welcomed within Erulius. Some of the original noble houses counseled moderation, suggesting that the principality would be better served by developing its considerable advantages rather than competing for imperial legitimacy. Their voices, however, were increasingly marginalized as Prince Kalden consolidated power and the Erulian population embraced the intoxicating vision of reclaiming imperial glory.

## Geography and Environment

The Erulian Principality occupies the continent's **southeastern quarter**, and it is geographically well-favored. Its entire eastern edge faces the **Azure Sea** — the maritime frontier that gives it Port Valen and the Erulian Navy. To the **north** it borders **Uratha**, the scholars' land, with Gunastran beyond that; to the **west**, its arid marches fall away into the **Balishan Desert** and the long-disputed border with Kaha'an; to the **south**, its territory narrows toward the peninsula and the approaches to Silaris. One fact of its position mattered nothing for most of its history and everything at its founding: Erulius lies about as far from **Vartonne** — the old western capital, clear across the breadth of the continent on the Western Sea — as any holding the empire ever granted.

The landscape is remarkably diverse:

- The Northern Reaches feature mineral-rich mountains with extensive iron, copper, and precious gem deposits  
- The Heartlands contain some of Elvandar's most fertile agricultural regions, with a moderate climate perfect for grain production  
- The Inland Woods remain largely untouched, housing ancient forests with rare timber and medicinal plants  
- The Western Marches are more arid, transitioning toward Kaha'an's desert, but contain valuable salt flats and quarries

Three major rivers flow through Erulian territory—the Ennor, the Tethian, and the Alder—providing both transportation networks and irrigation for the prosperous farmlands of the central province.

## Government Structure

The Erulian government operates as a feudal principality with imperial adaptations:

- **The Prince's Court**: Headed by Prince Kalden Erulius, a charismatic but intensely jealous ruler of thirty-five at the series' opening who has transformed the once-ceremonial position into one of actual power. His ambitions were ignited by The Rending, which he saw as divine providence creating an opportunity for Erulius to claim its rightful place.
    
- **The High Council**: Twelve noble houses that governed their regions semi-autonomously under imperial rule, now serving as a reluctant advisory body to the Prince. Several houses quietly maintain reservations about Kalden's imperial ambitions, preferring the principality's former focus on self-development.  
    
- **The Imperial Chancellery**: A bureaucratic structure deliberately modeled after the old imperial administration, staffed by ambitious civil servants who maintain records with obsessive precision. Created after The Rending, this institution represents one of Kalden's most significant governmental innovations.  
    
- **The Aspirant Guard**: An elite military force personally loyal to Prince Kalden, equipped with the finest Gunastran weapons and trained by Terinok advisors. Formed within the last decade, the Guard embodies the principality's new martial aspirations.

The capital city, Eruliath, was once a modest provincial center but has been aggressively expanded over the past two decades to create the impression of imperial grandeur. New construction deliberately mimics the architectural styles of both Balisha and Vartonne, often with exaggerated elements to suggest superiority.

## Economy and Resources

The Principality's greatest strength lies in its balanced and abundant natural resources:

- **Mining Operations**: The northern mountains contain some of Elvandar's richest iron deposits, along with copper, silver, and precious gems  
- **Agriculture**: The central plains produce surplus grain and vegetables that are exported throughout Elvandar  
- **Forestry**: The eastern forests provide high-quality timber, particularly valuable for shipbuilding and luxury furniture  
- **Manufacturing**: Recent partnerships with Gunastran have accelerated industrial development, though still lagging behind Gunastran's technological sophistication

The Erulian currency, the "Imperial Mark," features Prince Kalden's profile on one side and the old imperial seal on the other—a deliberate statement about the Principality's claims to legitimacy. This currency was introduced just fifteen years ago, replacing the more modest "Erulian Talent" that had been in circulation since the empire's collapse.

## Social Structure and Bondage System

Erulius maintains a complex social hierarchy that combines elements of imperial tradition with newer developments:

- **The Noble Houses**: The original twelve families that governed under imperial authority, now engaged in constant competition for the Prince's favor  
- **The New Imperial Court**: A growing class of administrators, diplomats, and military officers personally elevated by Prince Kalden, often from common origins  
- **Provincial Freeholders**: Independent farmers and merchants who maintain their own lands and businesses, taxed heavily but still prosperous  
- **Mining Conscripts**: A bound labor force that works in the northern mines under harsh conditions

The mining conscription system represents Erulius' form of bondage—not traditional slavery, but a form of indentured servitude with imperial justifications. Criminals, debtors, and political dissidents are sentenced to mine work for terms ranging from five years to life. While technically not inheritable, families of conscripts often fall into debt themselves, creating de facto generational servitude. The Principality presents this system as more "humane" than Balisha's slavery or Terinok's practices, pointing to the possibility of sentence reduction through exemplary service.

This labor system is justified through elaborate legal theory claiming it continues ancient imperial punitive practices, though historians from Uratha quietly note that the actual imperial system bore little resemblance to current Erulian practices.

## Culture and Daily Life

Erulian culture is characterized by a fascinating duality—a passionate embrace of perceived imperial traditions combined with genuine innovation:

- **The Aspirational Calendar**: Official holidays celebrate imperial milestones, with elaborate reenactments of historical events that grow more elaborate each year  
- **Fashion and Design**: Clothing styles deliberately combine elements from all imperial provinces, creating a distinctive aesthetic that is recognizably "Erulian" while claiming imperial heritage  
- **The Imperial Registry**: All citizens are encouraged to trace their ancestry to imperial connections, with government scribes available to "assist" in establishing such connections  
- **The Arts**: Heavy patronage of artists who create works celebrating Erulian connections to imperial legacy, with Silaris artists especially sought for commissioned pieces

Daily life for most Erulians is relatively comfortable compared to citizens of other successor states. Agricultural abundance ensures adequate food supplies, and mineral wealth funds public works. However, an undercurrent of anxiety runs through society—the desperate need to prove imperial worthiness creates constant pressure to demonstrate loyalty to Erulius' vision.

## Military Forces

The Erulian military represents one of the Principality's most substantial investments:

- **The Aspirant Guard**: Elite soldiers personally loyal to Prince Kalden, equipped with the finest imported weapons and armor  
- **The Provincial Legions**: Regional forces organized into units deliberately named after famous imperial legions  
- **The Erulian Navy**: A modest but growing fleet based at Port Valen, focused primarily on trade protection but increasingly capable of projection  
- **The Shadow Corps**: A secretive intelligence service operating both within Erulius and abroad, particularly active in Kaha'an

Military training incorporates techniques learned from Terinok advisors, but with distinctly Erulian flourishes intended to connect modern tactics with imperial military traditions. Military service offers one of the few paths for social advancement in Erulian society, with exceptional soldiers sometimes elevated to the New Imperial Court.

## Major Cities and Locations

### Eruliath

The capital city has been transformed from a provincial center into an aspiring imperial capital:

- The Amber Palace, home to Prince Kalden, deliberately constructed to outshine Vartonne's imperial structures  
- The Hall of Ancestors, a massive gallery featuring portraits (many recently commissioned) connecting current Erulian nobles to imperial figures  
- The Grand Bazaar, a commercial center designed to showcase Erulian products alongside imports from throughout Elvandar

### Port Valen

The Principality's primary seaport:

- Recently expanded harbor facilities that can accommodate larger vessels than any other port in Elvandar  
- The Naval Academy, training officers in both maritime commerce and warfare  
- The Foreign Quarter, where merchants from all successor states maintain trading houses

### Northcrest

The administrative center for the mining regions:

- The Assignation Court, where labor sentences are determined for mining conscripts  
- The School of Engineering, a recently established institution collaborating with Gunastran experts  
- The Redemption Halls, where conscripts who have served their terms receive their freedom documents

### Verdant Plains

The agricultural heart of the Principality:

- The Grain Exchange, coordinating distribution of agricultural products throughout Erulius and beyond  
- The Seasonal Temples, where harvest festivals maintain a connection to older imperial traditions  
- The Agricultural College, developing improved farming techniques that have significantly increased yields

## Current Political Tensions

The Principality faces several critical challenges:

- **Succession Uncertainty**: Prince Kalden has numerous potential heirs but has not officially designated a successor, creating intense competition among his children and other relatives  
- **Noble Resistance**: Several of the original twelve houses quietly resist the Prince's accumulation of power, maintaining their own networks and agendas  
- **Resource Exploitation**: The intensive mining operations are creating environmental problems in the northern regions that may prove unsustainable  
- **The Princess Problem**: Rumors of a surviving direct heir to the imperial throne represent an existential threat to Erulian claims of imperial connection

Additionally, the recent strengthening of ties with Terinok has created internal divisions, with some counselors advocating for a more balanced diplomatic approach while others push for even stronger military alliance against Balisha.

## The Imperial Museum

One of Prince Kalden's most ambitious projects is the Imperial Museum—a massive complex in Eruliath dedicated to preserving (and in many cases, recreating) artifacts from the Balishan Empire. The museum serves multiple purposes:

- Housing genuine imperial artifacts purchased, excavated, or occasionally stolen from other successor states  
- Displaying commissioned reproductions when originals cannot be obtained  
- Providing "educational" exhibits that subtly reinforce Erulian claims to imperial succession  
- Serving as a research center where scholars (carefully selected for their political reliability) study imperial history

The museum has become a key diplomatic tool, with foreign dignitaries invariably given extensive tours designed to impress upon them the depth of Erulius' imperial connections. Privately, scholars from Uratha who have visited the collection note the concerning tendency to display reproductions alongside genuine artifacts without clear distinction between them.

## International Relations

The Erulian Principality maintains complex relationships with the other successor states:

### With Balisha (Greed)

Their diplomatic status with Balisha is one of thinly veiled hostility. Both claim to be the true heir to the Balishan Empire, creating an imperial rivalry. The Principality uses its natural resources to challenge Balisha's trade dominance and forms alliances with Balisha's enemies while maintaining diplomatic protocols. Currently, there are increasing proxy conflicts in border regions between them.

### With Terinok (Wrath)

The Erulian Principality maintains a strategic alliance with Terinok. They receive military training and occasional support against Balisha, while providing resources in exchange for martial expertise. Their current objective is strengthening ties without becoming dependent, though cultural differences create frequent misunderstandings between them.

### With Silaris (Lust)

The Principality engages in cultural patronage with Silaris, commissioning imperial-themed art to bolster their legitimacy claims. They trade raw materials for luxury goods and artwork, with increasingly lavish patronage to compete with Vartonne's historical prestige. They've even established an Erulian Academy of Arts to rival Silaris itself.

### With Uratha (Pride)

The Principality maintains an academic alliance with Uratha, seeking scholarly validation of their imperial claims. They provide rare materials for Urathan research in exchange for educational access and fund historical research supporting the Erulian imperial lineage. However, there is notable tension due to frustration with Uratha's insistence on academic neutrality.

### With Vartonne (Sloth)

Their relationship with Vartonne is characterized by competitive contempt. They openly challenge Vartonne's imperial credentials while privately studying its protocols. The Principality recruits disillusioned Vartonne nobility and scholars, presenting itself as the vigorous, worthy alternative to Vartonne's faded glory. They've also adopted and "improved" many of Vartonne's imperial ceremonies.

### With Gunastran (Gluttony)

The Erulian Principality has a mutually beneficial partnership with Gunastran. They provide raw materials for Gunastran industry in exchange for finished goods and use Gunastran technology to develop their own resources. They engage in joint ventures in mining and resource extraction, though there is growing concern about the environmental impact on Erulian lands.

## The Principality's Vision

Prince Kalden's ultimate ambition goes beyond merely claiming connection to the imperial past—he envisions a reunified Elvandar under Erulian leadership. Unlike Balisha's greed-driven ambitions or Vartonne's nostalgia, the Erulian vision combines genuine administrative competence with an envy-fueled determination to prove their worthiness.

This vision manifests in practical policies:

- Diplomatic initiatives that emphasize pan-Elvandar cooperation (with Erulius naturally at the center)  
- Infrastructure development deliberately designed to accommodate future expansion  
- Educational systems that teach children about the glories of imperial unity while emphasizing Erulius' special role  
- Cultural programs that celebrate the distinctive contributions of all former provinces while suggesting their natural place under Erulian leadership

Whether this vision represents genuine idealism corrupted by envy or simply sophisticated propaganda masking naked ambition remains a matter of debate among observers from other successor states. What is clear is that the Principality's abundant resources combined with its psychological need to prove itself make it one of the most dynamic and potentially dangerous powers in post-imperial Elvandar.

The discovery of Princess Elara Valanar, the emperor and empress's daughter who remained safely within Kaha'an's walls during The Rending, would represent an existential threat to everything Prince Kalden has built over the past two decades. The Erulian Principality's entire claim to imperial legitimacy rests on being the closest surviving relatives to the throne. Her existence would transform them from potential heirs to mere distant relations, likely provoking immediate and potentially violent countermeasures once her existence becomes known.
