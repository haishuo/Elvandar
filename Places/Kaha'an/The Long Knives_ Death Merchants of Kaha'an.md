# The Long Knives: Death Merchants of Kaha'an

## Origins and Evolution

In a city where everything has a price, the Long Knives represent the ultimate commodity broker—they trade in death itself. This guild of assassins has been a shadow power in Kaha'an since before The Rending, growing stronger as the city's moral fabric frayed.

### Pre-Rending Foundations

- Originally formed during the late imperial period as a small covert organization  
- Operated primarily through discreet contracts among the nobility and wealthy merchants  
- Maintained strict protocols to remain invisible to imperial authorities  
- Specialized in eliminating targets without evidence or suspicion  
- Developed rigorous training methods focused on stealth, poison craft, and untraceable killing  
- Built a reputation for absolute reliability and discretion  
- Established their base in what would later become the Warrens

### Post-Rending Expansion

The chaos following The Rending created unprecedented opportunities for the Long Knives:

- Imperial law enforcement collapsed, removing their primary opposition  
- Demand for their services surged in the power vacuum  
- Territory expanded throughout the rapidly growing Warrens  
- Recruitment accelerated, bringing in desperate refugees with potential  
- Developed broader service offerings beyond simple assassination  
- Established information networks throughout all districts of Kaha'an  
- Transformed from a small specialized group to a major underground power

## Organization and Hierarchy

### The Mistress

At the apex of the Long Knives stands a figure known only as "The Mistress" or sometimes "Mother of Blades":

- Her true identity is known only to her most trusted lieutenants  
- Has led the guild for approximately three decades  
- Rumored to have never failed a contract in her active years  
- Now rarely performs assassinations personally, focusing instead on strategy and leadership  
- Commands absolute loyalty from her inner circle  
- Makes all final decisions on high-profile contracts  
- Holds the only veto power over contract acceptance  
- Maintains the guild's traditions and protocols with religious fervor  
- Has a secret familial connection to Rosik Kemvimore, as his mother, though this relationship remains hidden from all but a handful of individuals

### The Red Daggers

Directly below The Mistress are her elite enforcers and advisors, known as the Red Daggers:

- A council of seven senior assassins, each specializing in different methods  
- Each Red Dagger oversees a specific aspect of guild operations  
- All were personally trained by The Mistress at some point  
- Embody the highest level of skill attainable in the guild  
- Perform only the most sensitive or high-profile assassinations  
- Act as judges in internal matters and disputes  
- Can speak with The Mistress's authority when necessary  
- Each maintains a network of informants independent from the main guild structure

### Specialized Divisions

The Long Knives operate through specialized branches, each with distinct training and methodology:

#### The Stilettos

- Political assassins who specialize in eliminating targets with minimal evidence  
- Masters of poisons and methods that mimic natural deaths  
- Extensively trained in court etiquette and noble customs  
- Often operate under long-term cover identities in higher society  
- Primarily serve contracts from the noble houses and merchant cartels  
- Emphasize complete deniability in all operations

#### The Cleavers

- Specialists in public, message-sending assassinations  
- Execute targets in ways designed to intimidate rivals or allies  
- Experts in combat techniques rather than stealth  
- Often leave a signature mark to identify the killing as Long Knives work  
- Serve contracts where the client wants the source known, if not provable  
- Maintain a fearsome public reputation that enhances the guild's deterrent power

#### The Needles

- Poison specialists with extensive alchemical training  
- Maintain the guild's extensive collection of toxic substances  
- Develop new compounds for specific contract requirements  
- Often work in tandem with Stilettos on complex operations  
- Oversee a network of suppliers for rare ingredients  
- Control the guild's antidote reserves—a significant source of power

#### The Shadows

- Intelligence specialists who rarely perform actual assassinations  
- Maintain elaborate networks of informants throughout Kaha'an  
- Collect and analyze information on potential targets and clients  
- Vet contracts for complications or hidden agendas  
- Often serve as the initial point of contact for prospective clients  
- Ensure operations have complete intelligence before proceeding

### Training and Recruitment

The Long Knives maintain their quality and tradition through rigorous training processes:

- Most recruits are identified in childhood, often from the Warrens  
- Initial training focuses on physical skills and absolute obedience  
- Apprentices spend years learning under established guild members  
- Success rate from recruitment to full membership is less than one in ten  
- Those who fail but show promise are repurposed as informants or support staff  
- Those who fail completely disappear—no loose ends are permitted  
- Final initiation involves completing a solo assassination under observation  
- New members swear blood oaths with mystical and psychological binding elements  
- Advancement through ranks is based solely on demonstrated skill and loyalty

## Operations and Methods

### Contract Protocols

The Long Knives maintain strict protocols for all business transactions:

- Initial contact typically occurs through a network of seemingly unrelated businesses  
- Potential clients must provide certain codes or tokens to progress  
- Contract negotiation always involves multiple meetings in different locations  
- Payment terms are non-negotiable: half in advance, half upon completion  
- Contracts are sealed with ritual significance rather than written documentation  
- Contracts can be refused at The Mistress's discretion without explanation  
- Pricing scales with target difficulty, political sensitivity, and time constraints  
- Breaching contract confidentiality results in the client becoming a target themselves

### Signature Techniques

While adaptable to any situation, The Long Knives have developed certain trademark approaches:

- "The Last Breath" \- A poison delivered through a nearly invisible blowdart that mimics heart failure  
- "The Lover's Touch" \- Assassination during intimate encounters, leaving no signs of struggle  
- "The Ghost Step" \- Killing that occurs in supposedly secure locations, enhancing the guild's mystique  
- "The Mirrored Blade" \- Making deaths appear to be the work of rivals or enemies  
- "The Final Toast" \- Poisonings at public gatherings that create maximum political disruption  
- "The Red Signature" \- Public killings where the target is marked with the guild's symbol

### Safe Houses and Facilities

The Long Knives maintain extensive infrastructure throughout Kaha'an:

- Primary guild hall deep within the Warrens, its exact location known only to members  
- Training facilities disguised as ordinary businesses or residences  
- Network of safe houses in all districts, particularly in the Noble District  
- Secret passages and escape routes throughout the city  
- Hidden caches of weapons, poisons, and disguises accessible to members  
- Front businesses that provide both cover and legitimate income  
- Specialized facilities for poison brewing and weapon crafting

## Relationships with Power Players

### With the Four Cartels

The Long Knives maintain a complex relationship with Kaha'an's official power structure:

- Officially condemned by all four cartels in public forums  
- Secretly employed by all four cartels for various purposes  
- Each cartel maintains different channels and relationships with the guild  
- The guild carefully balances services to prevent any single cartel gaining advantage  
- Certain high-ranking cartel members have personal relationships with specific assassins  
- The guild maintains blackmail material on key cartel figures as insurance

#### The Grain: House Kemvimore

- Unique relationship due to Rosik's hidden past and familial connection  
- Communications occur through multiple layers of intermediaries  
- Most contracts involve eliminating potential business rivals  
- The Mistress and Rosik maintain absolute secrecy about their true relationship  
- Both recognize the mutually assured destruction their connection represents

#### The Water: House Larannas

- Tania Larannas employs the guild to maintain intelligence on rivals  
- Prefers to use them for information gathering rather than actual assassinations  
- Has established a reputation for honoring contracts impeccably  
- The guild respects her caution and precision in contract specifications

#### The Iron: Sa Ko Ren

- As a Terinok immigrant, initially resisted using the guild's services  
- Now employs them sparingly but effectively when diplomatic solutions fail  
- Values direct action over subtlety in contract specifications  
- The guild appreciates her straightforward approach to negotiations

#### The Slavers: Ronas Dermir

- Complicated relationship due to territorial overlap in the Warrens  
- Employs the guild frequently for settling personal vendettas  
- Often requests the most brutal, message-sending methods  
- The guild tolerates his crude approach due to consistent business  
- Both organizations maintain a wary respect for each other's capabilities

### The Uneasy Truce in the Warrens

The relationship between the Long Knives and the Slavers represents one of Kaha'an's most interesting power dynamics:

- Both recruit from the same population of desperate Warrens inhabitants  
- Territorial boundaries have been established through bloody negotiation  
- Each organization stays out of the other's primary business  
- Disputes are settled through formal meetings between representatives  
- Occasional joint operations occur when interests temporarily align  
- Both understand that open warfare would be mutually destructive  
- The Slavers particularly fear that any protracted conflict would drain their manpower  
- Such losses would weaken the Slavers' position against the other three cartels  
- Ronas Dermir recognizes that while the Long Knives might suffer in open battle, they excel at bleeding opponents through stealth and attrition  
- The delicate balance of power in Kaha'an leaves little room for such internal conflicts  
- This pragmatic coexistence embodies Kaha'an's approach to power—even enemies cooperate when profit demands it

## Cultural Impact and Reputation

### Myths and Rumors

The Long Knives cultivate certain myths to enhance their reputation:

- That they can reach anyone, anywhere in Kaha'an, regardless of protection  
- That The Mistress is immortal, having led the guild for generations  
- That certain members possess magical abilities enhancing their skills  
- That they never fail a contract once accepted  
- That they maintain a ledger of every kill performed in their history  
- That membership requires killing a loved one as final initiation  
- That they can resurrect the dead for a price no one has yet been willing to pay

While mostly exaggerated, these rumors serve a purpose—enhancing the guild's mystique and deterrent power.

### Social Perception

Different segments of Kaha'an society view the Long Knives through different lenses:

- The nobility publicly denounce them while privately employing their services  
- The merchant class views them as a necessary evil and business expense  
- The general population fears them as shadows that might strike at any moment  
- The Warrens inhabitants see them as one of the few potential paths to power  
- Foreign visitors are warned about them in hushed tones by concerned locals  
- Rival assassin organizations from other successor states maintain respectful distance

### As Embodiments of Kaha'an's Greed

The Long Knives represent a perfect manifestation of Kaha'an's embodiment of Greed:

- They commodify death itself, making it available for purchase  
- They value skills and services purely in terms of transaction  
- Their hierarchy is determined solely by ability rather than birth or connection  
- They maintain loyalty through profit-sharing rather than ideology  
- Everything—even betrayal—has its price in their value system  
- They represent the ultimate extension of Kaha'an's mercantile philosophy

## Current Activities and Evolution

### Expanding Beyond Assassination

In recent years, the Long Knives have diversified their services beyond simple killing:

- Intelligence gathering for political or commercial advantage  
- Specialized protection services for those who can afford them  
- Training in self-defense for wealthy clients concerned about rivals  
- Sabotage of business ventures or political initiatives  
- Recovery of sensitive information or valuable items  
- Verification of rumors or allegations for a price

### Internal Tensions

Despite their unified front, the Long Knives face internal challenges:

- Philosophical divisions between traditionalists and pragmatists  
- Debate over maintaining quality versus expanding operations  
- Questions about succession when The Mistress eventually steps down  
- Concerns about maintaining secrecy as the organization grows  
- Competition between specialized branches for resources and recognition  
- The tension of balancing service to all cartels without favoring any

### Future Prospects

As Kaha'an continues to evolve, the Long Knives face both opportunities and threats:

- The continuing centralization of wealth creates more potential clients  
- Growing tensions between cartels may increase demand for their services  
- Their information networks become more valuable as political stability declines  
- However, any shift in the balance of power could threaten their position  
- The possibility of imperial restoration would pose existential challenges  
- Their ultimate survival depends on maintaining the balance between visibility and secrecy

In a city defined by commerce, the Long Knives have made murder a business like any other—efficient, professional, and ultimately, profitable. They represent the darkest extension of Kaha'an's mercantile philosophy, where even death itself can be purchased for the right price.
