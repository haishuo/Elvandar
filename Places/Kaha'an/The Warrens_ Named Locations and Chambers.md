# **The Warrens: Named Locations and Chambers**

## **Overview**

This gazetteer catalogs the named chambers, passages, and landmarks throughout the Warrens. Not every chamber has a name—most are simply referred to by their general location ("the chamber at \-150 meters near the rust fixtures"). Names emerge organically when a location becomes significant enough to warrant distinction.

**Naming Conventions:**

* **Descriptive:** Based on physical features (The Hot Passage, The Squeeze)  
* **Functional:** Based on use (The Arena, The Forge)  
* **Historical:** Named after people or events (Fisher's Hall, Collapse Corner)  
* **Atmospheric:** Based on sensory qualities (The Well of Whispers, Echo Hall)

**Geographic Organization:** Locations are organized by depth level, then alphabetically within each level.

## **Surface Warrens (Ground Level to \-20m)**

### **The Bell Tower**

**Type:** Former temple bell tower  
 **Location:** The Sanctuary complex, visible landmark  
 **Description:** Stone tower rising 15 meters above the Surface Warren ruins. The bell was stolen years ago, but the tower remains a crucial navigation landmark visible across the northern district.

**Significance:** Primary meeting point reference ("Meet at the Bell Tower"). Used for giving directions throughout the Surface Warrens.

### **The Bone Market**

**Type:** Open-air market in ruins  
 **Location:** Northwestern section of Surface Warrens  
 **Description:** Market operating among the skeletal remains of collapsed pre-Rending buildings. Named for the exposed structural "bones" of ruined tenements that frame the market space.

**Goods:** Second-hand items, salvaged materials, questionable food, cheap tools. Lower quality than the Crossroads but more accessible.

**Population:** \~200-300 daily vendors and shoppers

### **The Breather Gates**

**Type:** Four major tunnel entrances  
 **Location:** Eastern Surface Warrens  
 **Description:** Four large entrances (3-5 meters wide) positioned to catch prevailing winds, creating natural air circulation. Named because standing here, you can "breathe" the best surface air before descending.

**Control:** Greyhands Gate Clan  
 **Traffic:** Heavy—primary route for food and goods

### **Collapse Corner**

**Type:** Intersection of ruined streets  
 **Location:** Southwestern Surface Warrens  
 **Description:** Where three pre-Rending streets meet, all lined with collapsed buildings. The corner building collapsed spectacularly during an early aftershock of the Rending, killing dozens. Memorial markers remain.

**Atmosphere:** Somber. Residents avoid it at night (claimed to be haunted).

### **The Dusty Steps**

**Type:** Wide stone staircase  
 **Location:** Northern Surface Warrens, leading to The Sanctuary  
 **Description:** Pre-Rending public stairs (40 steps) leading up to the temple. Constantly covered in desert dust despite regular foot traffic.

**Significance:** Ceremonial route for those seeking the Sanctuary's help. Climbing the steps has become symbolic gesture.

### **The Patchwork**

**Type:** Dense residential area  
 **Location:** Central Surface Warrens  
 **Description:** Sprawling area where residents have built makeshift structures from salvaged materials—wood, canvas, metal sheets—creating a "patchwork" of mismatched architecture. Most densely populated surface area.

**Population:** \~5,000-7,000  
 **Character:** Chaotic but communal. Strong neighborhood identity.

### **The Rust Garden**

**Type:** Open space with metal sculptures  
 **Location:** Eastern Surface Warrens  
 **Description:** Former plaza now filled with rusting metal salvaged from collapsed buildings. Residents have arranged pieces into accidental sculptures. Children play here, adults gather in evening.

**Atmosphere:** One of the few "beautiful" spaces in the Surface Warrens—unintentional art installation.

### **The Sanctuary**

**Type:** Former imperial temple, now refugee center  
 **Location:** Northern Surface Warrens (-20m below to surface)  
 **Full Description:** See Geography document  
 **Population:** \~150 permanent (monks, refugees), hundreds daily  
 **Controlled by:** The monks (neutral ground)

### **The Scaffold**

**Type:** Multi-level platform structure  
 **Location:** Western Surface Warrens  
 **Description:** Massive wooden scaffold built against a partially-standing pre-Rending building. Seven levels of platforms house \~400 people. Engineering miracle or disaster waiting to happen (opinions vary).

**Population:** \~400  
 **Stability:** Questionable but has survived 15 years

### **The Sink**

**Type:** Depression in ruins  
 **Location:** Southern Surface Warrens  
 **Description:** Area where ground subsided (possibly from tunnel collapse below), creating a bowl-shaped depression 30 meters across. Water collects here during rare rains. Becomes temporary market after water drains.

**Hazard:** Flooding risk during heavy rain

## **The Tiers (-20m to \-100m)**

### **The Ascent**

**Type:** Wide emergency escape route  
 **Location:** Connecting Tiers to surface  
 **Description:** 4-meter-wide passage with gentle slope (ancient emergency route). Well-maintained, well-lit by residents. The "main street" of the upper Warrens.

**Traffic:** Extremely heavy. The primary route for goods and people moving between surface and underground.

### **Brightness Hall**

**Type:** Medium chamber with functioning Lathion lighting  
 **Location:** \-40m, eastern Tiers  
 **Size:** 25 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Rare chamber where ancient blue lighting still functions brightly. Almost painfully bright compared to normal darkness. Used for work requiring good light.

**Population:** \~80 (craftspeople, those needing light for work)  
 **Rent:** Premium (worth it for the light)

### **The Clear Spring**

**Type:** Water station  
 **Location:** \-60m, central Tiers  
 **Size:** 30 meters diameter  
 **Description:** One of six major water stations. Named for exceptionally clear water—best purification system of all stations. Well Mothers' upper headquarters.

**Water Quality:** Excellent  
 **Population:** \~60 Well Mothers and support  
 **Control:** Well Mothers

### **The Copper Chamber**

**Type:** Large residential chamber  
 **Location:** \-70m, southern Tiers  
 **Size:** 45 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber notable for copper-colored mineral deposits on walls (not actual copper—disappointing to early residents). Three-level wooden construction inside houses \~400 residents.

**Population:** \~400  
 **Character:** Working-class residential. Strong community.

### **The Drop**

**Type:** Vertical shaft (natural feature, not Lathion-made)  
 **Location:** \-50m, western Tiers  
 **Description:** 30-meter vertical drop where ceiling of a lower chamber collapsed into upper passage. Crude rope ladders provide access. Dangerous but serves as shortcut.

**Hazard:** Multiple deaths from falls  
 **Usage:** Experienced climbers only

### **Echo Hall**

**Type:** Large chamber with unusual acoustics  
 **Location:** \-80m, central Tiers  
 **Size:** 40 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber where sound echoes dramatically. Speaking voice repeats 3-4 times. Musicians occasionally perform here (rare entertainment). Also used for public announcements.

**Population:** \~300  
 **Special Use:** Public gathering space, performances

### **Fisher's Hall**

**Type:** Medium residential chamber  
 **Location:** \-55m, northern Tiers  
 **Size:** 30 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Named after the Fisher family who claimed it first and have controlled it for 20 years. Three generations now live here. Name stuck even though many non-Fishers live here now.

**Population:** \~120 (including \~40 Fisher family members)

### **The Garden (Upper)**

**Type:** Small chamber with cultivated fungi  
 **Location:** \-65m, eastern Tiers  
 **Size:** 15 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber where residents successfully grow edible fungi using waste and darkness. Small but important food supplement. Carefully tended by community.

**Production:** Minor (feeds \~50 people)  
 **Significance:** Proof that some food production is possible underground

### **The High Market**

**Type:** Large commercial chamber  
 **Location:** \-45m, central Tiers  
 **Size:** 50 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Secondary market after the Crossroads. Better quality goods than surface markets but smaller selection than the Crossroads. "High" refers to elevation, not quality (though both are relatively high).

**Daily Traffic:** \~500-800 vendors and shoppers  
 **Control:** Long Knives (take transaction fees)

### **The Split**

**Type:** Large chamber divided by ancient wall  
 **Location:** \-75m, southern Tiers  
 **Size:** 50 meters diameter (divided into two 25m sections)  
 **Description:** Only chamber in the Warrens with an internal wall (Lathion construction—purpose unknown). Wall divides chamber into "Upper Split" and "Lower Split" (floor levels differ by 2 meters). Each section developed separate community.

**Population:** \~200 per side (\~400 total)  
 **Character:** Rivalry between sides (friendly competition)

### **Three-Ways Junction**

**Type:** Tunnel junction  
 **Location:** \-60m, connecting multiple passages  
 **Description:** Where three major passages meet at 120-degree angles. Natural gathering point and navigation landmark. Small market operates here.

**Traffic:** Constant  
 **Significance:** Major navigation reference point

## **The Midlevels (-100m to \-300m)**

### **The Arena**

**Type:** Large chamber repurposed for blood sports  
 **Location:** \-180m, central Midlevels  
 **Full Description:** See Geography document  
 **Size:** 50 meters diameter  
 **Population:** \~200 permanent (fighters, operators), 500-1,000 during events  
 **Control:** Independent operators paying tribute to multiple factions

### **The Breach**

**Type:** Catastrophic collapse cutting through multiple levels  
 **Location:** Vertical shaft from \-100m to \-400m  
 **Full Description:** See Geography document  
 **Hazard Level:** Extreme

### **The Crossroads**

**Type:** Major junction and marketplace  
 **Location:** \-150m, central Midlevels  
 **Full Description:** See Geography document  
 **Size:** 45 meters diameter  
 **Population:** \~400 permanent, 1,000+ daily visitors  
 **Control:** Neutral ground enforced by Long Knives

### **The Deep Pool**

**Type:** Water station  
 **Location:** \-250m, southern Midlevels  
 **Size:** 25 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Water station with unusually deep water reservoir visible below chamber floor (3 meters deep, crystal clear). Residents occasionally use it for swimming (rare luxury).

**Water Quality:** Good  
 **Population:** \~40 Well Mothers  
 **Control:** Well Mothers

### **The Fan Tunnel**

**Type:** Major ventilation shaft  
 **Location:** Vertical shaft at \-120m  
 **Description:** 4-meter-wide shaft with still-functioning ancient fan. The fan is massive (3 meters diameter) and turns slowly but powerfully. Standing beneath it, you can feel strong airflow. The rhythmic whooshing sound is audible throughout adjacent chambers.

**Traffic:** Moderate (used as passage despite fan)  
 **Hazard:** Don't touch the fan (lost limbs result)

### **The Forge (Main)**

**Type:** Industrial chamber with active forges  
 **Location:** \-140m, western Midlevels  
 **Size:** 35 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Primary Forge Rats operation. Five active forges operating (ventilation shaft overhead removes smoke). Hot, loud, smoky, but essential. Best metalwork in the Warrens produced here.

**Population:** \~60 Forge Rats  
 **Control:** Forge Rats (Spark's headquarters)  
 **Temperature:** 85-90°F due to forge heat

### **The Gallery**

**Type:** Long, wide passage with carved alcoves  
 **Location:** \-175m, connecting major chambers  
 **Description:** 100-meter-long passage with small alcoves carved into walls every 5 meters (Lathion design—purpose unknown). Residents use alcoves as tiny living spaces (barely 2x2 meters each). Called "gallery" because it resembles art gallery.

**Population:** \~150 (desperate people in alcoves)  
 **Character:** Extremely cramped poverty

### **The Garden (Lower)**

**Type:** Multiple small chambers with fungi cultivation  
 **Location:** \-160m cluster, eastern Midlevels  
 **Description:** Five interconnected small chambers (8-12 meters each) dedicated to growing edible and medicinal fungi. More sophisticated than Upper Garden. Community-managed resource.

**Production:** Modest (feeds \~200 people partially)  
 **Management:** Collective of experienced growers

### **The Grinding Stone**

**Type:** Medium chamber with mysterious Lathion fixture  
 **Location:** \-190m, northern Midlevels  
 **Size:** 30 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber dominated by large circular stone disc (3 meters diameter) that slowly rotates (ancient Lathion mechanism—purpose unknown). Residents use it to grind grain by placing it on the moving stone. Unintended but useful.

**Population:** \~100  
 **Special Use:** Grain milling (communal)

### **The Hot Passage**

**Type:** Tunnel section  
 **Location:** \-200m, southern Midlevels  
 **Description:** 50-meter passage section that's noticeably warmer than surroundings (80-85°F). Runs near geothermal source or active Lathion power conduit. Uncomfortable but useful for drying clothes/food.

**Usage:** Avoided except for specific purposes  
 **Hazard:** Dehydration if you stay too long

### **The Iron Hall**

**Type:** Slaver headquarters  
 **Location:** \-185m, central Midlevels  
 **Size:** 45 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Large, well-ventilated chamber serving as Ronas Dermir's headquarters and primary slave-holding facility. Single defensible entrance with heavy guards. Well-maintained (slavers are wealthy). Iron chains mounted to walls for restraining captives.

**Purpose:**

* Headquarters for Slaver operations  
* "Processing" center for captives (sorting, evaluation, preparation for sale)  
* Meeting place with surface buyers  
* Ronas's residence (has private quarters)  
* Well-ventilated and temperature-controlled (keeps "product" healthy)

**Population:** \~120 (Slavers, guards, captives)  
 **Control:** Slavers (Ronas Dermir)  
 **Hazard Level:** Extreme—heavily guarded

**Why This Location:** Ronas chose this chamber for strategic reasons:

* Accessible from surface (buyers can reach it)  
* Well-ventilated (captives don't die of heat)  
* Near major routes (easy transport)  
* Defensible (single entrance, well-guarded)  
* Comfortable (Ronas isn't going to sweat in the Depths)

### **The Lantern Chamber**

**Type:** Medium chamber, Lantern Boys gathering point  
 **Location:** \-155m, central Midlevels  
 **Size:** 25 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Where Lantern Boys gather between jobs. Named for the numerous lanterns hung here (always lit—guides need light to socialize even if they work in darkness). Informal headquarters.

**Population:** \~30 permanent, 100+ daily visitors  
 **Control:** Lantern Boys (neutral ground)

### **The Long Hall**

**Type:** Massive chamber (unusual shape)  
 **Location:** \-170m, eastern Midlevels  
 **Size:** 60 meters long, 15 meters wide, 5 meters high  
 **Description:** Unusually elongated chamber (Lathion design—purpose unknown). Residents built two-story structures along both sides, creating a covered "street." Feels more like a corridor than a chamber.

**Population:** \~400  
 **Character:** Busy commercial street-like atmosphere

### **The Pillars**

**Type:** Medium chamber with structural columns  
 **Location:** \-185m, western Midlevels  
 **Size:** 30 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Only chamber in service tunnels with free-standing pillars (four pillars supporting ceiling—Lathion construction). Pillars are 1 meter diameter, perfectly smooth brass. Residents built platforms between pillars creating unique multi-level space.

**Population:** \~150  
 **Architecture:** Most interesting residential chamber

### **Rust Route**

**Type:** Tunnel section  
 **Location:** \-130m, connecting passages  
 **Description:** 200-meter passage lined with rusty iron fixtures (Lathion hardware that has corroded—rare). Used as major navigation landmark. The rust provides texture for touch-navigation in darkness.

**Traffic:** Heavy (major route)  
 **Navigation:** Easy to follow by touch

### **The Silent Flow**

**Type:** Water station  
 **Location:** \-230m, eastern Midlevels  
 **Size:** 20 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Smallest water station. Named for unusual quiet—no echoing, no dripping sounds. Water flows silently from taps. Acoustically dampened (Lathion design—purpose unknown).

**Water Quality:** Good  
 **Population:** \~25 Well Mothers  
 **Control:** Well Mothers  
 **Atmosphere:** Eerie quiet

### **The Squeeze**

**Type:** Narrow passage section  
 **Location:** \-195m, connecting chambers  
 **Description:** 30-meter passage that narrows to barely 1 meter wide. Adults must turn sideways. Called the Squeeze for obvious reasons. Used as shortcut despite discomfort.

**Hazard:** Claustrophobic, risks getting stuck  
 **Usage:** Those carrying cargo avoid it

### **The Three Taps**

**Type:** Water station  
 **Location:** \-180m, central Midlevels  
 **Size:** 35 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Water station with three separate water sources/taps (different aquifer connections). Each has slightly different taste. Residents have preferences. Busiest water station due to location.

**Water Quality:** Variable by tap  
 **Population:** \~50 Well Mothers  
 **Control:** Well Mothers  
 **Daily Traffic:** \~2,000 people

### **The Waterfall**

**Type:** Natural water feature  
 **Location:** \-210m, eastern Midlevels  
 **Description:** Where ancient water pipe broke, creating constant stream flowing down wall into collection pool. Not drinkable without boiling but provides ambient sound and slight humidity. Considered beautiful by Warren standards.

**Atmosphere:** Rare pleasant sensory experience  
 **Population:** Nearby chamber (\~100) considered desirable

### **The Well of Whispers**

**Type:** Major water station with unique acoustics  
 **Location:** \-200m, central Midlevels  
 **Full Description:** See Geography document  
 **Size:** 30 meters diameter  
 **Population:** \~60 Well Mothers (headquarters of Grandmother Shira)  
 **Control:** Well Mothers

### **The Winding Stair**

**Type:** Spiral passage  
 **Location:** \-150m to \-250m  
 **Description:** 100-meter spiral passage descending between levels. Gentle slope makes it easier than vertical climbs but takes longer. Called "stair" though it's actually a ramp.

**Traffic:** Moderate  
 **Preference:** Those with cargo or difficulty climbing

## **The Depths (-300m to \-500m)**

### **The Black Pool**

**Type:** Flooded chamber, Slaver hunting ground  
 **Location:** \-380m, southern Depths  
 **Size:** Unknown (half-flooded)  
 **Description:** Chamber where ceiling partially collapsed, flooding lower half with dark water of unknown depth. Upper platforms remain accessible. Slavers use this as a hunting ground—desperate people hide here, easy to trap.

**Population:** \~20-30 (desperate people hiding, occasional Slaver patrols)  
 **Control:** Slaver hunting ground (not permanent base)  
 **Hazard Level:** Extreme (Slavers \+ drowning risk)

### **The Depths Warrens**

**General Description:** The Depths (-300m to \-500m) are where the Slavers primarily hunt, not where they live. Hot, dangerous, and filled with desperate people who lack faction protection. Slavers maintain several small temporary holding stations here but move all captives to the Iron Hall in the Midlevels for processing.

### **The Breathing Chamber**

**Type:** Small chamber with rhythmic sound  
 **Location:** \-420m, near Dead Man's Passage entrance  
 **Size:** 12 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber where the rhythmic pulse from the brass door is noticeably audible (once per \~3 seconds). Sounds like breathing or heartbeat. Deeply unsettling. Residents avoid it.

**Population:** 0 (abandoned)  
 **Atmosphere:** Horror movie waiting to happen

### **The Collector's Hold**

**Type:** Former name, now abandoned  
 **Location:** \-340m, central Depths  
 **Description:** Named for "the Collector"—an epithet Ronas Dermir carried in the early consolidation years, before the Warrens learned to call him the Slaver King. This was briefly his stronghold in those days, but he abandoned it long ago: too hot, too far from surface buyers, impractical for keeping captives alive. Now it's just an abandoned chamber, though the name persists in rumor.

**Population:** 0 (abandoned)  
 **Historical Note:** Residents still think this is Slaver headquarters (it's not—that's the Iron Hall in the Midlevels)

### **The Deep Garden**

**Type:** Failed fungi cultivation attempt  
 **Location:** \-360m, eastern Depths  
 **Size:** 20 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Attempt to grow food this deep failed (wrong temperature/humidity). Now filled with strange inedible fungi varieties. Weirdly beautiful but useless. Some fungi glow faintly.

**Population:** 0 (abandoned)  
 **Atmosphere:** Eerie bioluminescent forest

### **The Furnace**

**Type:** Extremely hot chamber  
 **Location:** \-390m, near geothermal source  
 **Size:** 25 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber where temperature reaches 105°F+ due to proximity to active Lathion power conduit in wall. Unbearable for extended time but useful for drying, heating, forging small items.

**Population:** \~20 (desperate people who tolerate heat)  
 **Usage:** Industrial—temporary work, not residence

### **The Hot Spring**

**Type:** Water station (warmest)  
 **Location:** \-380m, southern Depths  
 **Size:** 25 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Deepest water station. Water comes out warm (85°F) due to geothermal heating. Least pleasant water station—hot and humid—but essential for Depths residents.

**Water Quality:** Adequate  
 **Population:** \~30 Well Mothers  
 **Control:** Well Mothers  
 **Temperature:** 90-95°F ambient

### **The Lost Chamber**

**Type:** Partially collapsed chamber  
 **Location:** \-410m, northern Depths  
 **Size:** Unknown (mostly inaccessible)  
 **Description:** Chamber where collapse destroyed most of interior. Only small section accessible through rubble. Delvers occasionally dig here looking for artifacts. Dangerous.

**Population:** 0  
 **Delver Activity:** Periodic

### **The Mist**

**Type:** Chamber with steam  
 **Location:** \-370m, near Breach  
 **Size:** 30 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber where water leak meets hot air, creating constant mist/steam. Visibility near zero. Humidity extreme. Unpleasant but relatively safe (Slavers avoid it too).

**Population:** \~60 (hiding from Slavers)  
 **Atmosphere:** Fog-shrouded mystery

### **The Rust Tombs**

**Type:** Series of small chambers  
 **Location:** \-395m, eastern Depths  
 **Size:** Multiple 10m diameter chambers  
 **Description:** Six small connected chambers filled with ancient rusted equipment (Lathion machinery that failed). Resembles tomb or burial ground. Delvers strip it for metal.

**Population:** 0  
 **Delver Activity:** Heavy

### **The Scar**

**Type:** Collapsed passage section  
 **Location:** \-330m, cutting through multiple passages  
 **Description:** Where Breach collapse extended laterally, creating a "scar" of damaged passages. Unstable, dangerous, but several routes pass through it. Constant rockfall risk.

**Hazard Level:** High  
 **Usage:** Necessary but dangerous passage

### **The Shimmer**

**Type:** Small chamber with active Lathion lighting  
 **Location:** \-365m, central Depths  
 **Size:** 15 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber where blue Lathion lights flicker and pulse (malfunction?). Creates shimmering, hypnotic effect. Beautiful but unsettling. Some claim it causes hallucinations if you stay too long.

**Population:** \~30 (Ghostlights shrine)  
 **Control:** Ghostlights (spiritual significance)

### **The Watcher**

**Type:** Chamber with Lathion construct  
 **Location:** \-405m, western Depths  
 **Size:** 20 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber containing inactive(?) Lathion construct mounted in wall. Looks vaguely humanoid, 2 meters tall, brass and crystal. Residents swear it "watches" them. Actually part of Silent Wardens (automated repair system).

**Population:** \~25 (brave/desperate people)  
 **Atmosphere:** Deeply unsettling

**Type:** Chamber beyond the brass door  
 **Location:** End of the true path through Dead Man's Passage  
 **Full Description:** See Geography document  
 **Size:** 8 meters diameter

**Type:** Sealed ancient door  
 **Location:** End of the true path through Dead Man's Passage  
 **Full Description:** See Geography document  
 **Access:** Only Long Knives know the route

### **The Deep Dark**

**Type:** The labyrinth itself  
 **Location:** \-500m to \-700m  
 **Description:** Not a single location but the maze of passages forming Dead Man's Passage. Called "the Deep Dark" because it's the darkest, deepest place anyone goes (and returns from—barely).

### **The False Chamber**

**Type:** Dead-end chamber in the labyrinth  
 **Location:** Within Dead Man's Passage  
 **Size:** 15 meters diameter  
 **Description:** Chamber that seems like destination but is actually a dead end. Multiple passages lead here but none continue beyond. Many who enter Dead Man's Passage die here, thinking they've reached the end.

**Population:** 0  
 **Death Count:** Unknown but high

## **Passages and Routes**

### **Blue Light Way**

**Type:** Tunnel section  
 **Location:** \-165m, eastern Midlevels  
 **Description:** 80-meter passage where Lathion lighting still functions. One of few consistently lit passages. Used heavily despite being out of the way—light is worth the detour.

**Traffic:** Heavy

### **The Descent**

**Type:** Steep passage  
 **Location:** Connecting Tiers to Midlevels  
 **Description:** Steep (30-degree angle) passage dropping rapidly. Difficult to climb up, easy to descend. Major route but requires good footing.

**Traffic:** Heavy (despite difficulty)

### **The Narrows**

**Type:** Long narrow passage  
 **Location:** \-270m, connecting chambers  
 **Description:** 150-meter passage that's consistently 1.5 meters wide. Long, boring, claustrophobic, but direct route. Called the Narrows.

**Traffic:** Moderate

### **The Stairway**

**Type:** Actual stairs (rare)  
 **Location:** \-220m to \-280m  
 **Description:** One of few passages with actual carved stairs (Lathion construction). 60-meter descent over stairs. Much easier than climbing passages. Always crowded.

**Traffic:** Very heavy

### **Water Way**

**Type:** Wet passage  
 **Location:** \-145m, eastern Midlevels  
 **Description:** 100-meter passage where water constantly seeps through ceiling and runs along floor (ankle-deep). Cold, wet, unpleasant, but direct route. Boots essential.

**Traffic:** Moderate (people avoid if they can)

### **The Calling**

**Type:** Alleged phenomenon

**Type:** Urban legend  
 **Location:** Allegedly moves  
 **Description:** Legend of chamber that appears in different locations. Probably confusion in darkness, but story persists. "I found the moving chamber" means someone got hopelessly lost.

### **The Perfect Dark**

**Type:** Alleged location  
 **Location:** Unknown  
 **Description:** Myth of chamber where darkness is so complete it can be felt physically. Some claim it's calming, others say it drives you mad. Probably nonsense, but Ghostlights seek it.

### **The Weeping Wall**

**Type:** Real location with legend  
 **Location:** \-285m chamber  
 **Description:** Wall where water constantly seeps, creating pattern that resembles face. Legend says it's builder crying for their lost city. Actually just water seepage pattern.

## **Naming Conventions by Region**

**Surface Warrens:**

* Descriptive of physical features (The Scaffold, The Sink)  
* Historical references (Collapse Corner)  
* Functional (The Bone Market)

**The Tiers:**

* Optimistic names (Fisher's Hall, The Clear Spring)  
* Quality indicators (The High Market, Brightness Hall)  
* Physical features (The Split, Echo Hall)

**The Midlevels:**

* Mix of functional and descriptive  
* Major landmarks get proper names  
* Minor locations described by features

**The Depths:**

* Dark, ominous names (The Black Pool, The Collector's Hold)  
* Warning names (The Furnace, The Scar)  
* Survival-focused

**Dead Man's Passage:**

* All names reference death or danger  
* No optimistic names  
* Names serve as warnings

## **How Names Emerge**

**Organic Process:**

1. Someone uses descriptive phrase ("that chamber with the three water taps")  
2. Phrase gets shortened ("the three taps")  
3. Name sticks as more people use it  
4. Eventually becomes official neighborhood reference  
5. Appears in directions and navigation

**Deliberate Naming:** Some locations named intentionally:

* The Sanctuary (named by monks)  
* The Arena (named by operators)  
* The Crossroads (obvious function)  
* Well Mothers' stations (descriptive naming)

**Historical Names:** Some names preserve history:

* Collapse Corner (memorial)  
* Fisher's Hall (first residents)  
* The Breach (the event that created it)

## **Navigation References**

When giving directions in the Warrens:

**Common Format:** "Go to \[named chamber\], take the \[direction\] passage, follow it for \[landmark\], then turn at \[feature\]."

**Example:** "Go to the Crossroads, take the southern passage, follow it for five minutes until you hear the fan, then turn left at the warm wall."

**Named locations make this possible:** Without named chambers and landmarks, navigation would be nearly impossible to communicate verbally.

## **Future Growth**

New names emerge constantly as:

* Population expands into previously uninhabited areas  
* New chambers are discovered  
* Significant events create new landmarks  
* Communities develop distinct identities

This gazetteer represents current major named locations but is not exhaustive. Thousands of chambers exist; only the most significant earn names.
