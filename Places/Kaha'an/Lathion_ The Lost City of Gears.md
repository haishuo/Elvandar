# **Lathion: The Lost City of Gears**

## **What Is Lathion?**

Lathion, known in legend as "The City of Gears," is an underground elven metropolis built five thousand years ago beneath what would become Kaha'an. It represents the pinnacle of Lathionese civilization—a seamless integration of advanced technology, magical theory, and architectural mastery that has never been equaled.

**Status:** Abandoned for 5,000 years, sealed, but systems still functioning  
 **Location:** Beneath Kaha'an (unknown to surface dwellers)  
 **Size:** Approximately half of modern Kaha'an's surface area  
 **Population at Peak:** \~500,000 Lathionese elves  
 **Current State:** Perfectly preserved, waiting

**In Modern Times:** Lathion exists primarily as legend—like Atlantis or El Dorado. Some surface scholars speak of it as a mythical lost city of elves, supposedly somewhere underground, somewhere in the region. Most consider it pure fantasy. A few believe it might have existed but was destroyed millennia ago.

No one knows it lies intact beneath Kaha'an, still powered, still maintained by tireless machines, directly below the feet of 60,000 Warren refugees who live in its service tunnels.

## **Historical Context**

### **The Lathionese Civilization**

Five thousand years ago, the Lathionese elves were one of the most advanced civilizations on Elvandar:

**Technological Mastery:**

* Magi-tech engineering combining magic and mechanics  
* Geothermal power generation  
* Self-maintaining automated systems  
* Precision manufacturing and materials science  
* Architecture and construction beyond modern capability

**Magical Achievement:**

* Reality manipulation theories  
* Temporal mechanics understanding  
* Planar research and summoning  
* Magical information storage and retrieval  
* Integration of magic with technology

**Philosophical Sophistication:**

* Advanced mathematical and geometric understanding  
* Complex social organization  
* Emphasis on knowledge preservation  
* Long-term strategic thinking (planning in centuries)

**Relationship with Surface World:** The Lathionese maintained diplomatic relations with surface civilizations, including the early Balishan Empire. They were known but mysterious—elves who lived underground in a city of wonders, trading knowledge for resources, maintaining careful neutrality in surface conflicts.

### **Why Underground?**

The Lathionese chose to build underground for several reasons:

**Practical:**

* Access to geothermal power from the planet's core  
* Protection from surface conflicts and natural disasters  
* Stable environment (consistent temperature, humidity, protection from weather)  
* Isolation for sensitive research  
* Control over who could access their city

**Philosophical:**

* Symbolic descent toward the world's heart  
* Separation from surface political entanglements  
* Creation of controlled environment for perfect society  
* The underground as metaphor for hidden knowledge

**Strategic:**

* Defensibility (difficult to attack what you can't reach)  
* Secrecy (surface world knew of Lathion but not its extent)  
* Independence from surface resource chains  
* Ability to seal themselves away if necessary

### **The Decision to Seal**

Five thousand years ago, the Lathionese made a momentous decision: they sealed their city and vanished from history.

**What Is Known (from records):**

* The sealing was deliberate, not forced  
* The population participated willingly  
* The brass door was closed from inside  
* Service tunnels were left accessible (suggesting intention to preserve them)  
* The city's systems were set to continue operating indefinitely  
* All inhabitants entered the Core chamber for a final act

**What Happened (See "The Rending" document):** The complete story involves the Destiny Engine, temporal mechanics, and an act of unprecedented sacrifice. This information is covered in detail in the Rending document, as it's intrinsically connected to that catastrophe.

**The Result:** Lathion ceased to be an active civilization but remained physically intact. The city's automated systems continued their maintenance routines. The brass door remained sealed. And 5,000 years passed in silence.

## **Lathion in Legend**

### **Surface World Knowledge**

**Among Scholars (especially Uratha):** Lathion appears in ancient texts as:

* A mythical underground city of elves  
* A place of great learning and technology  
* Supposedly located "somewhere beneath the earth" in the region  
* May have been real, probably destroyed or abandoned  
* Comparisons to Atlantis—legendary, possibly metaphorical

Most scholars consider it interesting mythology, not historical fact. A few believe it existed but was lost millennia ago. Almost none believe it could still exist intact.

**Among Common People:**

* Fairy tale city where elves lived underground  
* Place of magic and wonders (in children's stories)  
* "Lost like Lathion" \= vanished completely  
* Generally considered pure fantasy

**Among Warren Residents:** Most have never heard the name. They know they live in ancient tunnels built by "the builders" thousands of years ago, but they don't connect this to legendary Lathion. The two concepts exist separately in cultural consciousness.

### **The Long Knives' Secret**

The Long Knives know more than anyone else in the Warren:

**What They Know:**

* A massive brass door exists deep in Dead Man's Passage  
* The door is ancient and perfectly preserved  
* It's sealed by mechanisms they don't understand  
* Something important lies beyond it  
* The route to the door must be protected

**What They Don't Know:**

* The door leads to Lathion proper  
* What Lathion is or was  
* That Lathion is an intact, functioning city  
* How to open the door  
* Why it was sealed

**The Mistress's Strategy:** She treats the brass door as strategic asset and insurance policy. Eventually, someone will want what's beyond that door. When that day comes, the Long Knives will have leverage. She's playing a long game with stakes she doesn't fully understand.

## **The Wonder and the Mystery**

### **What Makes Lathion Remarkable**

**Still Functioning After 5,000 Years:** This is the most extraordinary fact about Lathion. The city's systems continue operating:

* Power generation  
* Air circulation  
* Water purification  
* Lighting  
* Temperature control  
* Self-repair automatons  
* Rotation mechanisms  
* Security systems

No modern civilization has achieved this level of durability and reliability.

**Perfect Preservation:** The city appears recently abandoned, not ancient:

* No decay or deterioration  
* Clean streets and buildings  
* Functional equipment  
* Intact records and artifacts  
* Personal belongings undisturbed

The self-repair systems have maintained everything as if the inhabitants will return tomorrow.

**Knowledge Repository:** The Great Repository contains information that could revolutionize modern understanding:

* Technologies centuries beyond current capability  
* Historical records predating the Balishan Empire  
* Magical theories and techniques  
* Scientific knowledge across all disciplines

For Urathan scholars, this represents the ultimate prize—knowledge beyond imagination.

**The Destiny Engine:** At Lathion's heart lies a device of unprecedented power and purpose. Its exact nature and current status are detailed in The Rending document, as understanding the Engine requires understanding what happened 5,000 years ago and 20 years ago.

## **Access and Discovery**

### **The Path to Lathion**

**Requirements:**

1. Know the service tunnels exist (surface dwellers don't)  
2. Descend into the Warrens (dangerous, authorities don't go there)  
3. Navigate Dead Man's Passage (lethal maze, only Long Knives have the map)  
4. Reach the brass door (few have seen it)  
5. Open the brass door (no one knows how)  
6. Enter Lathion proper (unknown what awaits)

**Barriers:**

* **Social:** The Warrens is officially ignored, entering is admission it exists  
* **Political:** The Long Knives control access to Dead Man's Passage  
* **Physical:** The maze is deadly without the map  
* **Magical:** The brass door requires specific magical resonance to open  
* **Knowledge:** Must know the door exists and how to open it

**Who Could Potentially Access:**

* Princess Elara Valanar (has the bloodline and potential knowledge)  
* Urathan scholars (if they learned the door's location and requirements)  
* The Long Knives (if they discovered how to open it)  
* Anyone with the right combination of knowledge, capability, and determination

### **Discovery Scenarios**

**Controlled Discovery:** A knowledgeable party (Elara, with proper preparation) deliberately seeks Lathion, opens the door carefully, and explores methodically. Knowledge is revealed gradually, dangers are managed, decisions are made thoughtfully.

**Forced Discovery:** Political upheaval or crisis drives someone to seek Lathion desperately. The door is opened without full understanding. Consequences are unpredictable and potentially catastrophic.

**Accidental Discovery:** Someone stumbles upon the door's opening mechanism by chance. Lathion is revealed unexpectedly. Multiple factions race to claim it. Chaos ensues.

**Gradual Revelation:** The door opens but provides access only to the antechamber initially. Further access requires solving puzzles, gathering keys, or demonstrating specific qualities. Lathion reveals itself slowly, testing those who enter.

## **The Warning**

Lathion was sealed deliberately by a civilization that understood far more than modern Elvandar. They chose to close the brass door, set their machines to eternal maintenance, and vanish from history. This was not defeat or disaster—it was choice.

**Why That Matters:** The Lathionese possessed:

* Technology beyond modern understanding  
* Magical capability exceeding current practice  
* Thousands of years of accumulated knowledge  
* Long-term strategic thinking

And they chose to seal themselves away.

**The Question:** If a civilization that advanced decided their city should be sealed, should it be opened?

**The Counterargument:** Perhaps they sealed it FOR the future—preserving knowledge for when the world would be ready. The systems continue functioning, suggesting intention to preserve rather than destroy.

**The Uncertainty:** Without understanding why the Lathionese made their choice, opening Lathion is a gamble. It could represent humanity's greatest achievement or most catastrophic mistake.

## **Lathion's Legacy**

Even sealed and forgotten, Lathion impacts the world:

**The Warrens:** 60,000 people survive because Lathionese ventilation and water systems still function. The service tunnels support a refugee city. Ancient engineering provides shelter, air, and water to those the surface world abandoned.

**The Architecture:** Perfectly smooth walls that cannot be modified. Brass fixtures that still gleam after millennia. Systems that work without understanding. The Lathionese built to last forever—and they succeeded.

**The Mystery:** A city of wonders lies beneath Kaha'an, unknown and untouched. It waits in darkness while the world above struggles with problems the Lathionese solved five thousand years ago.

**The Promise:** That one day, someone will open the brass door. Someone will descend into Lathion proper. Someone will access the Repository and understand what was lost.

**The Question:** Will that be Elvandar's salvation or its doom?

## **Summary**

Lathion stands as testament to heights once achieved and deliberately set aside. It represents both humanity's potential for greatness and the wisdom to know when greatness should be restrained.

The city waits—powered, maintained, preserved—for whatever comes next. Its machines continue their eternal tasks, indifferent to the passage of millennia. The brass door remains sealed, requiring knowledge and capability that has not existed for five thousand years.

Above it, 60,000 refugees live in its service tunnels, surviving on systems they don't understand, maintained by machines they've never seen, in passages built for purposes they cannot imagine.

And somewhere, the possibility exists that someone—Princess Elara, perhaps, or another with the right combination of blood and knowledge—could turn the key, open the door, and reveal to the modern world what the Lathionese chose to hide.

Whether that would be wonder or catastrophe remains to be seen.
