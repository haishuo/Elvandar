# Languages

This folder holds the linguistic history, language systems, naming rules, scripts, lexicons, and
translation notes for Elvandar. Language follows people here: a phonology or etymology is not canon merely
because it sounds plausible. It must fit the culture that speaks it, the history that transmitted it, and
the prose in which the reader encounters it.

## Creative authority

Language development is **generative, not reconstructive**. History and culture determine who speaks,
who meets, what institutions preserve language, and what kinds of registers must exist. They do not make
the existing sound-forms into a puzzle the language must solve.

The working order is:

1. Settled history, culture, characterization, and narrative function establish the language's speakers
   and communicative needs.
2. A design plate creates the language itself: sound, word shape, grammar, derivation, vocabulary, and
   naming machinery.
3. The author iterates that plate by ear and by feel until it is approved.
4. Only then is the existing corpus compared with the approved language, using the protection ladder
   below.
5. P0 forms receive a plausible place in the language history at any cost. P1 forms are preserved whenever
   the language can carry them naturally. P2–P4 forms receive no accommodation merely because they were
   written first.

Existing prose determines both **change cost** and a strong presumption of survival. It does not determine
linguistic validity. A P1 form should remain when inheritance, borrowing, dialect, or ordinary historical
change explains it without special pleading. If keeping it would deform the language, the form changes and
its full prose blast radius is conformed.

## Name protection

The following P0 forms—and only these forms—are protected by the author's rulings of 2026-08-21 and
2026-08-22:

- **Xion**
- **Elara**
- **Valanar**
- **Kemvimore**
- **Rosik**
- **Kaha'an** — including its use as the transferable “Royal Seat” title
- **Arol Batae** — the complete institutional name “Old Guard”; *arol* means “old” within it

The approved language history must accommodate these spellings and pronunciations, but it need not make
them ordinary native formations. A protected name can be archaic, foreign, dynastic, learned, or
phonologically exceptional for a demonstrated historical reason. Its current proposed etymology is not
protected.

P1 forms already present in chapter prose are nearly fixed. Design should avoid changing them whenever a
natural historical route exists, but they may change rather than force one-off sounds, fake etymologies,
or exceptions that exist only to rescue them. Everything below P1 is free design space. Such forms may be
ignored while the language is built and should be replaced outright when their register contradicts the
people who supposedly named them.

**Explicit exception — former *Lathion*.** On 2026-08-22 the author removed the city-name from protection
and authorized its replacement despite its Book 3 prose footprint. The language was designed without
accommodating that placeholder; the approved history then generated native **Halauren / Halauri** and
modern Balishan **Hauren / Haureni**. The controlled migration is complete. *Lathion/Lathionese* survive
only where a document explicitly discusses the retired labels.

## Working method

For each language or historical stage:

1. Establish the speakers: geography, livelihood, institutions, kinship, religion, class, literacy, and
   contact with other peoples.
2. Establish the historical period. Three thousand years of Open Hauren cannot be treated as one
   unchanging language, and Imperial Balishan cannot be projected unchanged into every successor state.
3. Build one coherent design plate around P0 forms and without consulting P2–P4 names: phonology,
   phonotactics, grammar, productive morphology, a seed lexicon, and naming systems. P1 forms are soft
   constraints: test them, but do not build exceptions solely to save them.
4. Stress-test the plate on newly composed words, names, dialogue, legal language, and ordinary speech.
5. Iterate or reject the plate on its own merits. Rejected plates remain design history, not canon.
6. After approval, audit the attested register. Accommodate every P0 form; preserve P1 whenever reasonable;
   let P2–P4 forms survive only when they naturally belong. Rename failures after checking and conforming
   every dependency.
7. Derive daughter languages and historical sound changes from the approved system, then expand the
   lexicon as narrative need requires.

## Narrative constraint

The existence of a conlang does not grant characters access to it. In particular, characters do not know
Halauri proper names merely because the author has coined them, and Xion's narration cannot supply a
translation he does not possess. Author-facing endonyms and inscriptions must remain off the page until a
POV character has a legitimate route to understanding them.
