# Currencies of Elvandar

**Status:** Author-approved names and monetary relationships, 2026-10-03. Approval is bounded to this document; it does not approve the surrounding working language plates in full.

## National currencies

| Nation | Currency | Pronunciation | Approved naming route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balisha | **jenel** | JEN-el | Early Imperial *jen*, “worked metal; alloy,” + instrumental/medium *-el*: a standardized metal payment piece. The components are approved in Early Imperial Balishan Plate 1; their monetary application is approved here. |
| Uratha | **zhenel** | ZHEN-el | Inherited imperial *jenel*, with *j → zh*. This bounded word development is approved without promoting all of Modern Urathan Plate 1. |
| Vartonne | **jenel**, formally **Imperial jenel** | JEN-el | Conservative imperial treasury spelling and a claim of continuity. It is distinguished in trade as the *Vartonnian jenel*. |
| Erulius | **denar** | DEN-ar | Ordinary currency predating Kalden's special imperial issue; wages, provisions, prices, and taxes are reckoned in denars. Proposed origin: an old counting term transferred to the principal coin. Native root and derivation remain to be designed. |
| Gunastran | **drask** | DRASK | Proposed historical route: an assayed metal portion named in workshop speech, later adopted by the mint. Native derivation remains to be designed. |
| Terinok | **tarn** | TARN | A shared old weight-name used across cantons; canton stamps certify their issues against the common standard. Native derivation remains to be designed. |
| Silaris | **serel** | SER-el | Proposed historical route: an inherited mint-name transferred to its coin. The mint's location and the linguistic ancestry remain to be designed. |

The sound-forms are approved. Proposed deeper etymologies are research/design briefs, not completed native-language derivations. In particular, do not reverse-engineer “coin” from *Thal'krev, thal'zhuum*: the phrase is attested, its internal analysis is not settled.

The old imperial coin is the **jenel**. Related names in successor states do not establish interchangeable coins, equal value, or a common modern monetary authority. Vartonne's currency remains depreciating. Uratha gains no money trade; Terinok continues to require advance payment and refuse paper on principle. Erulian wealth remains extractive, not evidence of a continental financial centre.

## Kalden's Imperial jenel

Kalden's special issue is officially styled **Imperial jenel** by his own government. It bears his profile on one face and the old imperial seal on the other. Introduced fifteen years before Book 1, it is a high-value coin with a declared value in denars; the exact ratio is not yet set. It does **not** replace the denar or ordinary Erulian small coinage.

His choice of the old imperial coin-name asserts restoration and succession. The coin belongs to the same credentialing programme as the Amber Palace, Imperial Chancellery, Aspirant Guard, and Hall of Ancestors. Acceptance as payment does not endorse his title. Merchants can distinguish it as the **Erulian jenel**.

**Imperial Mark** and **Erulian Talent** are retired labels, preserved only in editorial history. The former account in which the Mark replaced the Talent is superseded by the ordinary-denar/special-jenel distinction.

## Elara's official Imperial jenel

Once Elara is fully established, the imperial mint in **Kaha'an** strikes **Imperial jenels bearing her face**. These are the **official Imperial jenels**: she is the actual reigning empress and they are issued under her authority by the imperial mint. Kalden's use of the same name asserts a title he does not hold; it does not confer equal imperial legitimacy. Vartonne's inherited use likewise records its claim of continuity, not equivalent authority over her issue.

The minting is an ordinary act of her government. It need not be a deliberate answer to Kalden. For him, the contrast is intolerable: his portrait asserts a claim, hers records who reigns. This constrains characterization if the coinage enters a scene; no particular confrontation, dialogue, or chapter placement is required here.

Official status does not automatically establish superior bullion content, exchange value, or universal foreign acceptance. The existing account of Kalden's coin as sound money belongs to the period before Elara's full establishment; later relative values must follow the economics.

## Denominations and prose

Accounting units, physical denominations, and colloquial coin-names are distinct. Small-coin names, metal contents, denomination ladders, exchange rates, and the exact date of Elara's issue remain open. Uratha's decimal counting system does not by itself establish decimal currency subdivisions.

“A copper,” “a silver coin,” and similar existing prose remain valid everyday descriptions. Janice's demand for one copper is not renamed by this ruling. Currency vocabulary is introduced where the speaker or POV needs to identify the unit or issuer; no blanket substitution through the chapters is authorized.
