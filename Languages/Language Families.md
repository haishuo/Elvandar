# Language Families

**Status:** Deep relationship between Halauri and Balishan settled 2026-08-22. The second ancient
surface family has the author-facing working label **Ancestral Continental**; its internal branches and
eventual endonym remain open.

## The central relationship

Imperial Balishan is genealogically descended from spoken Halauri. It is not an unrelated human
language carrying only a large layer of prestigious Halauri loans.

During Hauren's nearly three open millennia, human communities learned, used, and eventually nativized
the Halauri contact varieties employed in trade, diplomacy, technical instruction, worship, and
record-keeping. The resulting **Surface Halauric** varieties became community languages rather than
permanent second-language approximations. Human children acquired them natively, and human speech habits
changed them.

The cultural mechanism is established in `Halauri Culture.md`: sustained traffic through the Great Shaft
and Crown's Diplomatic Quarter, a teachable institutional contact register, bounded technical instruction,
and growing human-to-human use across markets, workshops, records, and households.

**Current design authority:** `Design Plates/Late Open Halauri - Plate 1.md` and `Design
Plates/Surface Halauric Bridge - Plate 1.md` are the working generative proposals; approved `Design
Plates/Early Imperial Balishan - Plate 1.md` is the fixed daughter target. The older reconstruction files
named below retain cultural history and candidate mechanisms, but their linguistic details are not canon
unless the design plates recover them.

Those communities did not begin without language. Their older **Ancestral Continental** speech supplied a
deep substrate: sounds, constructions, names, and ordinary vocabulary survived inside the adopted
Halauric language. Balishan therefore has two ancient sources without having two equal genealogical
parents. Its descent is Halauric; its human substrate is structural and lexical rather than decorative.

The contact and substrate sound systems are in `Late Open Halauri.md` and `Ancestral Continental.md`.
Late Open public speech preserves clear penultimate-stressed vowels, hiatus, /θ/, /ɕ/, and /ʔ/; the common
human substrate profile favors initial stress, vowel reduction, clusters, stronger codas, and mergers of
those three unfamiliar consonants. `Early Surface Halauric.md` now orders their interaction, establishes
the first Marresonnian/Vartonnian isoglosses, and derives learned **Xion** /ˈzaɪ.ən/.
`Grammar Foundations.md` supplies the parallel structural history: Late Open verb-final civic syntax and
final role marking yield to substrate SVO order and prepositions, while the shared modifier-before-head
order survives intact.

`Halauric Core Lexicon.md` passes six controlled reconstruction tests. LOL *onna* “established settlement”
becomes the documentary **-onne** head; mixed CON *vart* + *onna* produces *Vartonne*, while LOL *marreth*
+ *onna* regularly becomes inherited Marresonnian *Marresonne*. The imperial cluster then distinguishes a
whole Hieratic title (*Kaha'an*), human learned formations (*Kaha Batae, Arol Batae*), and a
learned–inherited hybrid (*Arol Rhutan* < LOL *re-hutan*). Finally, substrate CON *fen* “prepare plant
remedies; herbal medicine as skilled work” survives unchanged as an ordinary Balishan activity, craft,
and practitioner noun, while substrate CON *sar* survives as a genderless respect title before names and
bynames. English *Master* and *Mistress* translate that one form rather than two Balishan genders.
The first medical register then contrasts substrate *len* “healing; healer” and service-site *lenum*
“clinic” with learned LOL *sathen* “physician”; parallel *fenum* distinguishes an apothecary workplace
from practitioner *fen*. The household test adds substrate *dal* “functioning household” and mixed
Imperial *seldal* “chartered House,” with learned legal *sel-* classifying the older human social body;
fossil *-annas* explains *Larannas* without becoming a productive noble suffix.

`Imperial Balishan.md` now carries the branch through its political hinge. The 500 BR transfer moves a
portable court and access economy rather than Vartonne's entire population; common imperial grammar meets
Marresonnian speech at the coast while Vartonne retains archives and procedural authority. This produces
modern Balishan/Kaha'ani, Vartonnian, and Urathan as related but institutionally different daughters.
Their first shared derivational evidence is polity-relational ***-an*** in *Balishan* and *Urathan*;
eastern settlement-relational ***-i*** in *Kaha'ani* belongs to the coastal capital branch and marks a
narrower urban identity.

## The split

```text
Open-period Halauri
├── metropolitan Halauri inside Hauren
│   └── Sealed Halauri
│       └── Terminal Halauri
│
├── late open textual/contact standard
│   └── Hieratic Old Halauri on the surface
│       └── copied, taught, cited, and continually reinterpreted
│
└── human-nativized contact varieties
    └── Surface Halauric + Ancestral Continental substrate
        └── Proto-Balishan heartland continuum
            ├── Vartonnian varieties
            ├── Marresonnian varieties
            └── Early and Late Imperial Balishan
                ├── modern Balishan, centered on urban Kaha'ani
                ├── Urathan
                ├── modern Vartonnian
                └── other imperial daughter varieties not yet assigned

Ancestral Continental
├── substrate retained inside Surface Halauric and its descendants
└── independent branches not yet identified
```

The diagram is genealogical shorthand, not a claim that each split happened once or cleanly. Hauren was
open for almost three thousand years. Different surface communities may have shifted at different dates
and from different Open Halauri registers, producing multiple inherited layers before the sealing.

## What the Latin analogy means

The analogy works at several levels:

| Historical role | Elvandaran counterpart |
|---|---|
| Spoken Latin becoming community vernaculars | Human-nativized Surface Halauric |
| Romance languages descended from Latin | Proto-Balishan, Imperial Balishan, and their later daughters |
| Classical or ecclesiastical Latin preserved after ordinary speech changes | Hieratic Old Halauri |
| Learned Latin borrowings entering Romance languages after their inheritance | Imperial learned vocabulary and deliberate Urathan re-Halaurization |
| Pre-Latin and later non-Latin vocabulary within Romance languages | Ancestral Continental substrate and later contact loans |

The analogy does not require surface humans to become culturally elven or claim elven ancestry. Language
shift can follow prestige, technical access, trade, education, administration, religion, intermarriage,
and ordinary bilingual community life. Whether biological Halauri ancestry also entered particular
human lines remains a separate open question.

## The three Halauri histories after contact

### Living Halauri inside the city

The metropolitan language continues changing after the sealing and eventually becomes Terminal
Halauri. It is a collateral relative of Balishan, not Balishan's immediate parent and not a frozen form
of the language humans once learned.

### Hieratic Old Halauri on the surface

Late Open Halauri supplies a conservative textual and ceremonial norm. Human schools copy, pronounce,
teach, reinterpret, and add to it for millennia. It plays the role of a classical and ecclesial language,
but its surface tradition still has a history.

### Surface Halauric

The spoken contact varieties nativized by humans continue changing without native metropolitan contact.
By the time Valan founds the empire roughly a millennium after the sealing, Vartonnian and Marresonnian
speech are daughter varieties in their own right. Imperial standardization begins from those living
languages, not from an attempt to make an entire population speak manuscript Halauri.

## The two-root lexicon

Every Balishan form will eventually be classed by route, not merely by whether it sounds ancient:

1. **Inherited Halauric:** passed through ordinary Surface Halauric speech and transformed by regular
   sound change.
2. **Ancestral Continental substrate:** retained from human speech during and after language shift.
3. **Learned Old Halauri:** borrowed from texts, rites, law, scholarship, or deliberate imperial
   archaism after the inherited form had already changed.
4. **Neo-Halauri:** coined later from prestigious roots, especially in Collegiate Urathan, without
   necessarily ever having existed in native Halauri.
5. **Later contact vocabulary:** borrowed from Terinoki, Gunastran, Far Eastern, and other languages as
   historical contact requires.

This permits inherited/learned doublets. A single Open Halauri root can survive as an ordinary
Balishan word, return in a conservative legal form, and be borrowed yet again as an Urathan scholarly
coinage. Terminal Halauri may carry a fourth cognate changed along its own sealed history.

## `Proto-Balishan` remains a period label

The existing label remains useful for the pre-imperial heartland continuum. It now means **the late
Surface-Halauric varieties ancestral to Imperial Balishan**, heavily shaped by Ancestral Continental
substrate. It no longer means an unrelated human family with a merely Halauri upper register.

Forms currently catalogued as Proto-Balishan are not automatically assigned to one root source. *Fen,
sar, len,* and site *-um* are now established as Ancestral Continental substrate; *Kalden* and other
established forms may ultimately prove inherited Halauric, substrate, mixed, or later innovations. Their
labels date them and place them; the ordered sound history constrains their etymologies, and `Grammar
Foundations.md` constrains their possible structures. *Vartonne, Marresonne, Fen, sar, lenum,* and *fenum*
now have actual derivations; learned *sathen* has a controlled LOL route; the remaining forms still require
roots.

## Boundaries deliberately left open

- Whether Ancestral Continental was one language, a close family, or a modern reconstruction imposed on
  several related varieties
- Which independent modern languages descend from it
- Whether Terinoki, Old Gunastran, or Erulian belongs anywhere inside that deep family
- When particular human communities shifted to Surface Halauric
- How much grammar, sound structure, and core vocabulary survived from the substrate
- The degree of mutual intelligibility among Vartonnian, Marresonnian, and other pre-imperial varieties
- The degree of difference between Hauren's settled contact register and metropolitan household speech
- The amount, if any, of biological ancestry shared between Halauri and surface populations

Until those peoples are built, no desire for a tidy two-family map may decide these questions in advance.
