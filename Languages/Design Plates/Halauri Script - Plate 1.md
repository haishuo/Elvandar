## 1. The authorized architecture

Halauri uses one related script tradition rather than an ordinary alphabet beside an unrelated magical
code. Its monumental letters are **runes**: compact geometric signs designed to survive carving in stone,
brass, ceramic, and other durable media. Documentary and rapid workshop hands simplify and join those
forms without changing the alphabetic system.

Each monumental rune has two conventional values:

1. a **phonetic value**, used to write ordinary language;
2. an **operative value**, used in exact technical notation to route and constrain Arcane Magic.

The operative value is not released by the sound and does not make ordinary literacy magical. It belongs
to an exact rune installed in a complete working. A technically valid sequence that also spells a
meaningful Halauri word is an author-facing **runeword** or **bound word**. The native term remains to be
generated after the plate's structure wins approval.

This is magical programming, not reality-commanding language. The host device supplies the thing acted
upon; the rune sequence supplies operations and control; a caster, stored source, or larger system supplies
power.

## 2. What “runic alphabet” means

### Ordinary monumental writing

- The script is a full alphabet: consonants and vowels are written.
- Late Open public writing normally runs **left to right**, with lines ordered top to bottom.
- Monumental words are divided by a shallow centered wedge; documentary hands use visible spacing.
- **th, dh, sh,** and author-facing **x** each represent one rune, not sequences of Roman letters.
- The glottal stop has its own rune. The apostrophe in *Kaha'an* therefore transliterates a consonantal
  letter, not native punctuation.
- Consecutive vowel runes write hiatus. A glottal rune appears only where /ʔ/ is actually pronounced.
- The chart gives monumental citation forms. It does not yet establish the documentary and commercial
  hands, punctuation beyond word division, or Terminal Halauri shapes after two millennia of sealing.

### Exact operative writing

Operative forms preserve the monumental rune's topology but add requirements ordinary carving does not
meet: calibrated proportions, controlled depth, conductive or responsive material, keyed joins between
signs, and a complete binding frame. A handwritten rune is as inert as a sketch of a gear.

The visual relationship is nevertheless close enough that an untrained reader can mistake:

- a machine graduation for a letter;
- a technical array for a sentence;
- an ordinary label for part of the working it describes;
- or one layer of a combined inscription for the whole.

That ambiguity supports the existing Brass Door scene without changing its answer. The marks on the Door
resolve as teeth, bearings, and graduations rather than a hidden spell. The scholars initially read them as
text because Halauri text, notation, and machinery genuinely share a designed geometry.

## 3. Rune-names are short

Rune-names are conventional one-syllable forms, normally clipped from the familiar word that supplied the
old acrophonic name. They behave like *ay, bee,* or *zed*, not like ceremonial invocations. Roman digraphs
can make a name look longer than it sounds: ***Thum*** /θum/ is still one syllable and three phonemes.

This brevity is practical as well as aesthetic. Artificers audit arrays aloud, instructors dictate safe
sequences, and maintenance crews call tesserae to one another while both hands are occupied. A sequence
such as ***Lit–Ir–Tir–Ar*** can be spoken cleanly under workshop noise; a chain of full lexical names could
not. Most rune-names therefore contain two or three phonemes, with three-letter romanizations strongly
preferred and no productive ceremonial expansion.

The clipped names are restricted technical vocabulary. Their existence does not make every corresponding
human daughter-language root a direct survival of the rune-name, and it does not replace the fuller Late
Open word in ordinary speech.

## 5. Visual construction

The first monumental forms use a common rectangular field and a small grammar of strokes:

- a long **stave** establishes the sign's axis;
- a closed angular tooth marks a stop;
- open paired cuts mark frication;
- a short counter-stroke distinguishes the voiced partner where the inventory has one;
- sonorants use continuous paths or paired staves;
- vowels are balanced freestanding forms rather than consonantal staves;
- the glottal rune is the only deliberately broken central stave.

The family should read as **precision cut into brass**, not borrowed Elder Futhark. Exact forms are shown
in the linked SVG. The chart is a first visual candidate: topology can change on visual review before the
inventory becomes canon.

## 6. Ordinary words, technical arrays, and bound words

These are three related but non-identical objects.

| Object | Linguistic reading | Operative reading | Active? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary inscription | Yes | None intended | No |
| Technical array | Often partial or none | Valid operators, relations, and channel labels | Only when framed, hosted, and powered |
| Bound word | A meaningful Halauri word | A valid operator sequence matching the host's purpose | Only when framed, hosted, and powered |

Most technical arrays are not words. They use rune cells, grouping cuts, channel lines, numerals, and
nonalphabetic measurements to make their operation unambiguous. Bound words are admired because they are
rare compressions: the public label and operative instruction agree closely enough that a trained worker
can read, remember, audit, and replace the sequence as one unit.

The linguistic meaning does not cause the magic. It helps human minds verify the machine. A bound word can
be more efficient than a prose-length array because its operator chain closes with fewer transitions and
less leakage, not because the universe is impressed by a pun.

## 7. Activation grammar

A working requires all four layers below.

### 1. Host

The receiving object or system declares what the operations address: a door, lamp, conduit, archive form,
weapon, climate loop, or other prepared mechanism. The same rune sequence can be invalid in a host whose
channels do not supply the required kinds of matter, energy, duration, or spatial relation.

### 2. Binding frame

A closed nonalphabetic frame distinguishes an executable sequence from a label. It supplies:

- start and termination;
- reading order;
- grouping and scope;
- connection to the host's channels;
- and safe failure routes for an invalid or interrupted sequence.

Breaking the frame normally makes the word inert. Breaking it while charge is moving can release whatever
the frame was containing.

### 3. Exact joins

Small keyed cuts between runes determine whether values are read as consecutive phases, nested conditions,
parallel channels, or alternatives. They are the operative equivalent of syntax and punctuation. Copying
the rune shapes while omitting their joins copies vocabulary without grammar.

### 4. Charge

The host must receive power from a mage, cooperative casting team, stored crystal, environmental source,
or larger distribution system. Runes neither create energy nor grant mastery of a magical school. They
make supplied forces act in a repeatable relation.

## 8. Four first bound-word tests

The examples below use ordinary Late Open roots because the strongest bound words make their public and
operative readings reinforce one another. The capitalized rune-name sequences are author-facing analysis,
not the way Halauri prose is normally printed.

`Halauri Bound Words - Plate 1.md` expands this first test into a governor-initial civic frame, seven
candidate words, shared operator families, and controlled one-rune substitutions. Nothing in that dependent
plate promotes this working inventory without separate approval.

### *lita* — “open”

```text
L        I          T       A
OPEN  ·  IDENTIFY · SEVER · PRIOR
Lit      Ir         Tir     Ar
```

In a prepared door host, ***lita*** declares an access cycle: identify the presented key or authorized
person, separate the barrier when the condition succeeds, then restore its prior closed state. Fire can
supply movement, Earth the material relation, Water the persistence and reset, and the host's geometry the
target. The word cannot create those channels merely by spelling them.

This is the flagship example because the public label *OPEN* is also a compact operating instruction.

### *bata* — “guard; interpose”

```text
B         A       T       A
GUARD  ·  PRIOR · SEVER · PRIOR
Bat       Ar      Tir     Ar
```

In a ward host, ***bata*** compares an approach against the established safe state, interposes when the
boundary is violated, severs the hostile connection, and restores the ward's baseline. It does not decide
who is hostile; an authorization or identification channel must already supply that distinction.

### *rena* — “return; restore”

```text
R          E           N      A
RETURN  ·  CONDITION · BIND · PRIOR
Ren        Esh         Nav    Ar
```

In a maintenance host, ***rena*** returns a system to a recorded baseline when a declared condition is
met, then binds the restored state long enough for ordinary controls to resume. This is suitable for
self-resetting valves, lifts, alignment tools, and safety cutoffs; it cannot repair matter the host lacks
the Earth channel or stored pattern to reconstruct.

### *thuma* — “maintain; keep operational”

```text
TH          U        M          A
SUSTAIN  ·  TARGET · EXCHANGE · PRIOR
Thum        Ur       Mer        Ar
```

In a climate or power host, ***thuma*** sustains a designated output by exchanging current inputs against
an established baseline. It is a maintenance loop, not perpetual motion. It consumes stored or supplied
power, and a depleted source ends the working.

These four tests are intentionally mundane. A script used by half a million citizens should first keep
doors, wards, lifts, air, and utilities functioning. Weapon enhancement is a possible host, not the reason
the system exists.

## 9. Modular rune tesserae

Haureni maintenance systems can carry operative runes on standardized palm-sized **tesserae** of brass,
ceramic, crystal, or composite material. A tessera fits into a keyed rail and exposes one rune plus its
permitted joins. Workers reconfigure a compatible host by installing a documented sequence, testing it
without charge, closing the frame, and only then admitting power.

This is the closest Elvandarian equivalent to socketed runes:

- one tessera supplies one operator;
- the ordered set forms an array or bound word;
- the receiving device determines the domain;
- the joined frame determines syntax;
- the power source determines scale and duration.

Not every rune fits every rail. Physical keying prevents the most elementary category errors, while
restricted Arcanum hosts permit combinations a civic maintenance rail refuses. A cracked, recut, or
improvised tessera can defeat those protections—which makes salvaged sets valuable and experimentation
dangerous without making random combinations reliably spectacular.

## 10. Relationship to the four Arcane schools

The runes are **operators**, not a second set of elemental schools.

| Arcane domain | What the host supplies | What runes can control |
|---|---|---|
| Fire / energy | Force, motion, heat, electricity, stored charge | routing, release, feedback, repetition |
| Earth / matter | substance, composition, hardness, physical pattern | selection, binding, exchange, restoration |
| Water / time | duration, previous state, temporal persistence | sustain, record, return, reset |
| Air / space | position, distance, boundary, target relation | open, close, channel, transfer |

A two- or three-school array still requires a caster with the relevant mastery, several coordinated
casters, or a host whose channels were prepared by specialists. Reading ***lita*** does not teach Air
Magic; owning its four tesserae does not provide Fire to move a door.

This explains why Hauren can automate combination casting without making modern multi-school mastery
common. The expertise has been embodied in devices, materials, keyed channels, and tested sequences.

## 11. True Magic and the non-negotiable ceiling

No rune encodes love, worthiness, sacrifice, motive, or divine creative will. True Magic is not a fifth
channel available to sufficiently clever notation.

- The Divine Artifacts can contain rune-governed Arcane functions, but their judgment of motive is not a
  runeword that can be copied.
- The Destiny Engine can use immense operative arrays to coordinate its matter, energy, temporal, and
  spatial systems. The arrays do not supply the freely given lives or make the universe accept the edit.
- A transcription of the Engine's controls therefore cannot recreate the Engine's act. At best it is the
  control logic for machinery whose impossible source and circumstance are absent.

The distinction preserves the existing cosmology: Arcane Magic can be formalized because it manipulates
forces within reality; True Magic remains an expression of divine fragments that no notation commands.

## 12. Why the Destiny Engine looks hasty

Ordinary Haureni architecture integrates operative geometry into the object being built. A door's array
belongs to its frame; a climate word sits inside a distribution plate designed with it; repairs preserve
the visual whole.

The Destiny Engine was built in months. Its runic control layer should therefore look modular and urgent:

- standardized tessera rails bolted onto finished structures;
- repeated bound words where a mature design would use a single integrated array;
- exposed channel joins and redundant safe-failure frames;
- correction plates laid over abandoned routings;
- ordinary civic components repurposed at unprecedented scale;
- and later additions visibly less elegant than the ancient chamber into which they were installed.

The runes help explain how Hauren could build something unprecedented so quickly: they possessed a mature
modular control language. They do not erase the haste. They make the haste legible.

## 13. Historical transmission

### Surface inheritance

Surface communities most readily inherit the public/documentary alphabet. Their copies preserve rune
topology but simplify proportions, change stroke order, and adapt letters to human sounds. Ordinary writing
survives because it needs ink, teaching, and readers. Operative writing degrades because it also needs exact
geometry, joins, hosts, materials, and trained access to magical channels.

This permits all of the following to be true together:

- Hieratic Old Halauri preserves conservative letter-forms and spellings;
- Surface Halauric and Proto-Balishan develop living documentary descendants;
- surface workshops copy procedures and isolated arrays without understanding the full theory;
- Gunastran standardized sigils descend from debased Halauri operator practices;
- Urathan scholars can read public inscriptions while remaining unable to operate an Arcanum matrix;
- and modern people can reproduce the *shape* of a rune without reproducing its working.

### Sealed and Terminal Halauri

Two thousand years of isolation permit the spoken language, documentary hand, and operative notation to
continue changing inside Hauren. The open-period inventory is therefore a bridge, not a claim that every
Terminal inscription uses the same shapes or meanings unchanged. Safety-critical civic hosts can preserve
old words indefinitely even after their ordinary pronunciation becomes archaic.

Repository encoding remains beyond alphabetic runewords. Its faceted forms can use spatial relations,
light, material response, and attunement as simultaneous syntax. A citizen able to read a street sign or
replace a ***lita*** rail is not thereby able to query the Repository.

## 14. Existing prose test

The plate requires no prose change.

- In *Wonder*, “numerical or phonetic or both” is exactly the uncertainty produced by a related alphabetic
  and technical tradition.
- The Brass Door's apparent inscription still resolves into machinery, preserving the chapter's reveal.
- In *Inhabitants* and *Neighbours*, scholars can read and debate public/foundational inscriptions without
  thereby controlling the systems around them.
- In *Bequest*, the Repository forms remain “not text” in any ordinary page-bound sense.
- Elara's residue lets her recognize weight and relation without translating the runes or acquiring
  technical competence.

The author-facing plate does not grant Xion, Elara, or the narrator native rune-names. Any story use still
requires a legitimate discovery, translation, or scholarly route.

## 15. Approval boundary

The **architecture** authorized on 2026-09-02 establishes that:

- Halauri's monumental alphabet is runic;
- the wider related script tradition includes documentary and technical hands;
- exact monumental runes possess phonetic and operative values;
- rune-names are short conventional call-signs, normally one syllable and two or three phonemes;
- ordinary writing is inert;
- valid arrays require a host, binding frame, exact joins, and charge;
- bound words are meaningful Halauri words that also form valid operative sequences;
- modular tesserae can install those sequences in compatible Haureni devices;
- runes control Arcane Magic but cannot encode or compel True Magic;
- and the Destiny Engine can use hurried modular runic controls without deriving its power or sacrifice
  from them.

Approval of the **inventory** would additionally establish the twenty-seven citation forms, short
rune-names, operative values, the first four bound words, left-to-right public direction, and their working
transmission consequences. Until the author approves that visual and linguistic layer, those details remain
the first candidate rather than canon.

This plate does not yet establish the documentary or workshop glyph set, native punctuation, Foundational
Halauri forms, Terminal Halauri innovations, Repository syntax, a complete magical programming language,
or any new story-visible artifact.
