# Foundational City Name — Plate 1

**Status:** APPROVED — canonical native city-name suite  
**Created:** 2026-08-22  
**Approved:** 2026-08-22  
**Retired project label:** *Lathion*; controlled repository migration completed 2026-08-22

This plate generates the **native name-family** of the five-thousand-year-old elven city from its founding
event. It does not preserve any sound, segment, meaning, or rhythm from *Lathion*. It no longer assumes
that the native name is also the form spoken in Book 3: the reader-facing name must be generated separately
by the human languages that borrowed and wore down this endonym.

## 1. What the founders were naming

The first name predates the sealing by roughly three thousand years and the Rending by nearly five
thousand. It therefore cannot mean “sealed city,” “lost city,” “tomb,” “final sacrifice,” or anything else
that predicts later history. It **can** mean refuge if refuge was the founding reason rather than a later
condition.

Why the founders went underground is deliberately unknown. No surviving surface account identifies the
danger, homeland, political rupture, magical event, religious command, or environmental pressure—if any—
that drove them. A name can preserve the fact that they sought shelter without preserving what they sought
shelter from. The absence of the second half is history, not a blank to fill speculatively.

The founders were naming a deliberate civilizational undertaking:

- a city built downward in seven interdependent levels;
- an engineered habitat rather than a settlement that accumulated accidentally;
- a practical descent toward geothermal power;
- a philosophical and possibly religious movement toward the world's heart;
- a controlled civic environment intended to sustain ordinary life, knowledge, making, trade, and
  government together;
- an independent city that nevertheless remained open to the surface for three millennia.

The strongest semantic center is therefore **life deliberately carried beneath shelter**. The engineering
and heartward descent describe how the refuge became a civilization; they need not have been its first
cause. The name acquires later resonances—the sealing, the Warrens sheltering another refugee population,
and the city's final sacrifice—but none is present as prophecy in the founding meaning.

## 2. Which historical form the reader knows

The name whispered in Book 3 is a **human legendary descendant**, not an ancient pronunciation recovered
by archaeological miracle. It need not be the exact word spoken at the founding, the ordinary Late Open
endonym, or the everyday Terminal endonym.

The required chain is:

> Foundational civic name → Open-period native descendants → late contact borrowing → early human
> vernacular descendant → Imperial Balishan form → modern legendary Balishan/Kaha'ani form

Three thousand years of native life occur before the city seals. Another two thousand years then separate
the last living contact from Book 3. A perfectly unchanged five-thousand-year-old form would be less
plausible than a deeply transformed descendant.

A second, learned route may coexist with the vernacular one:

> Late Open written form → Hieratic manuscript tradition → Urathan or antiquarian restoration

That route can preserve **Halauren** or a still older spelling long after ordinary Balishan has produced a
different city-name. The result would be a historical doublet: two names with one source but different
histories, social registers, and degrees of sound change.

## 3. Minimal older machinery used by this plate

This is not a complete Foundational-language reconstruction. It introduces only enough older material to
test city names against the working Late Open plate.

Provisional Foundational features:

- uvular **q** /q/, lost from the Late Open inventory;
- contrastive long vowels, later broken into the clear vowel sequences favored by Late Open speech;
- unstressed central **ë** /ə/;
- final vowels retained natively but often lost in human Surface-Halauric borrowing;
- a fossil civic suffix **-ta**, “a collective undertaking instantiated as a place”;
- an older settlement suffix **-ēna**, “a chosen habitation founded by the movement of a community.”

Candidate regular developments:

1. initial **q** weakens to /ʔ/ and is then lost in ordinary Open-period speech;
2. unstressed **ë** is lost or colored by an adjacent vowel;
3. intervocalic **t** lenites to Late Open **th** /θ/;
4. old long or glide-bearing vowels become written hiatus in Late Open;
5. the final vowel of the old habitation suffix is lost as the suffix ceases to be productive, producing
   the Late Open proper-name ending **-en**;
6. human borrowers move stress toward the first lexical root and contract hiatus;
7. later Surface-Halauric, Imperial Balishan, and modern daughter changes remain to be generated by their
   own plates rather than guessed backward from a preferred modern result.

No change exists solely for one protected outcome: the old city-name is not protected. These rules survive
only if they also work when Foundational vocabulary is expanded.

## 4. Candidate A — the Halauren native suite

### Founding form and meaning

**\*Halā-qur-ēna** /ha.laː.quˈreː.na/

- **\*halā** — living continuity: not abstract biological life alone, but a community carried across
  generations; the regular ancestor of Late Open **hala**, “life; living body; live”;
- **\*qur** — beneath cover, withdrawn from exposure, or held inside protection;
- **\*-ēna** — a chosen habitation founded by a community's movement, especially a resettlement.

The whole means **“the habitation where life was carried under shelter.”** The compact author translation
is **“Life Under Shelter”;** the historical reading is **“the Refuge of the Living.”** Neither says what
threatened that life. By Late Open times the old suffix and the full force of *qur* are no longer productive,
so the name feels inherited rather than like an ordinary transparent phrase.

### Transmission

| Stage | Form | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Foundational | **\*Halāqurēna** /ha.laː.quˈreː.na/ | Refuge-settlement name used by the founding community |
| Early Open | **Hala'urena** /ha.la.ʔuˈre.na/ | /q/ weakens to /ʔ/; long vowels lose contrast |
| Middle Open | **Halaurena** /ha.la.uˈre.na/ | The old glottal is lost inside the inherited proper name; hiatus remains |
| Late Open | **Halauren** /ha.laˈu.ren/ | The fossil habitation ending erodes to **-en**; native hiatus and late stress remain |
| First human loan | **Halauren** /ˈha.lau.ren/ | Surface speakers preserve the written shape but contract hiatus and move stress root-initially |
| Modern vernacular | **Hauren** /ˈhau.rən/ | Generated by approved Surface Halauric Bridge Plate 1; canonical modern form |
| Modern learned | **Halauren** or older **Halaurena**, provisional | Possible manuscript restoration rather than ordinary inheritance |

Thus **Halauren** is the old name modern scholars might print, not automatically the word Xion knows. The
receiving-language history generates canonical modern **Hauren**, with **Haureni** for the people,
language, and adjective.

### People and language

The city-name and the native people-name descend from the same older compound with different historical
endings:

- **Halauren** — the Late Open native city-name and possible learned modern restoration;
- **Halauri** — one or more members of its people; invariant plural in author-facing English;
- **Halauri** — also the provisional native adjective and language-name;
- **Foundational / Early Open / Late Open / Sealed / Terminal Halauri** — author-facing historical stages;
- **Surface Halauric** — the human-nativized family ancestral to Balishan, using *-ic* only as an
  author-facing family label where *Halauri* would be ambiguous.

These labels let the author discuss the lost civilization while the modern forms are being designed. They
do **not** predetermine what ordinary Balishan speakers call the city, its people, or their language. A
modern demonym may descend from the vernacular city-name while **Halauri** survives as a learned term.

> The Halauri had walked these roads for fifty centuries.

> The precision was Halauri.

### Character

The **Halauren native suite** is the leading candidate. It permits the city to have begun as an actual
refuge while refusing to manufacture an answer about the danger. The name-family then accumulates meanings
without requiring its sounds to stand still: refuge becomes open metropolis; the open metropolis seals
itself for an unrelated or forgotten reason; its service works shelter the Warrens; and the last Halauri
die saving people outside the refuge their ancestors built. No character needs to explain that density on
the page.

## 5. Candidate B — Oremaith

**Provisional source:** **\*Qorë-mai-ta**, “the Heartward Work.”  
**Late Open:** **Oremaitha**.  
**Late Open native form:** **Oremaitha**; native people/adjective **Oremaithi**.  
**Human legendary descendant:** uncoined.

This remains the strongest engineering-and-philosophy candidate. It describes the mature civilization
beautifully, but it says less about why anyone undertook the descent in the first place.

## 6. Candidate C — Nomaereth

**Provisional source:** **\*Noma-weri-ta**, “the ordered world made inward.”  
**Late Open native form:** **Nomaeritha**; native people/adjective **Nomaeri**.  
**Human legendary descendant:** uncoined.

This candidate treats the city as a deliberately complete world within the world. It has a colder,
philosophical register and fits the controlled environment, but it describes an accomplished system rather
than a frightened or purposeful founding movement.

## 7. Approved result and boundary

The author approved **Halauren / Halauri** as the canonical native suite on 2026-08-22. Surface Halauric
Bridge Plate 1 stress-tests the transmission on unrelated words and generates the human loan through
approved Early Imperial Balishan into canonical modern **Hauren / Haureni**. The unknown threat remains
unknown: the native name establishes that the founders understood the city as shelter, not what drove them
to seek it.

This approval establishes the ancient native name suite and the naming event. It does not approve the
entire Foundational language beyond the machinery used here. The separately authorized controlled pass
subsequently performed the prose and canon migration.
