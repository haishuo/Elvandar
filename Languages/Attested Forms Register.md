# Attested Forms Register

**Status:** Corpus and blast-radius inventory, reclassified 2026-08-22. `TGMS - OLD/` is excluded.
P0 forms are mandatory design constraints; P1 forms are strong but defeasible constraints; P2–P4 forms
are material to test **after** a language is designed, not evidence the design must explain.

`Names.md` remains the authoritative cast register and collision guide. This file asks a different
question: **where will an approved replacement have to be conformed if a current form loses?** Exposure
measures editing cost and the degree of resistance a form receives. Proposed routes and meanings below are
preserved as the history of the earlier reconstruction plate; none is binding unless independently
approved in the new generative process.

**Current generative status:** `Design Plates/Modern Balishan - Plate 1.md` now supplies a working,
unapproved Modern Balishan system generated before P2–P4 comparison. It retains the approved Early
Imperial grammar, affricates, and diphthongs; distinguishes ordinary, urban Kaha'ani, and learned court
registers; and generates the first native terrain, infrastructure, and layered place-name machinery. Its
P0 and preliminary P1 comparisons are tests only. No generated word, name, or proposed corpus route gains
authority until the author approves the plate or a bounded part of it.

## Exposure and stability

| Code | Meaning | Design and conformance rule |
|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Explicitly protected form | The form survives at any cost. The language must supply a plausible native, historical, foreign, learned, or exceptional route. |
| **P1** | Present in chapter prose | Preserve whenever a natural route exists. Change only when survival would contort the system; then conform prose, drafts, skeletons, generated HTML, and downstream references. |
| **P2** | Load-bearing future principal, recurring planned form, or settled offstage history | No linguistic design authority. It can change freely in design terms, though continuity still requires a careful pass through plans, sheets, and dependent arcs. |
| **P3** | Beat draft, outline, sheet, place dossier, or other supporting canon only | No design authority. Usually cheaper to replace than P1–P2. |
| **P4** | Naming-guide example, rejected candidate, folk etymology, or construction aid | No design authority and little or no conformance cost. |
| **R0** | Explicitly authorized replacement despite prose exposure | The existing form supplies blast radius only. It has no design authority; approve the replacement before conforming prose and downstream canon. |

These are both protection and conformance tiers. A one-scene prose name is P1 because it is on the page; a
future protagonist may be P2 despite being far more important. P0 compels the language to bend. P1 resists
change but loses before the language is forced into special pleading. P2–P4 can be ignored during design,
and a register-inappropriate form should be replaced rather than rationalized. No form is renamed by this
inventory alone. Revision begins only after a design plate is approved. Earlier changes such as
*Valorian* → *Hadris* belong to the superseded reconstruction workflow; the replacement itself receives no
special protection and will face the same post-approval audit as every other non-P0 form.

## Route labels

The route column records what must eventually be tested:

- **INH** — inherited through Surface Halauric into a Balishan-stage language;
- **SUB** — retained from the Ancestral Continental substrate;
- **HIE** — borrowed from the Hieratic Old Halauri textual tradition;
- **NEO** — coined later in a consciously Halauri-looking prestige register;
- **CON** — borrowed from or native to another known language family;
- **MIX** — compounded or reshaped from more than one layer;
- **UNK** — evidence does not yet choose among routes;
- **TR** — an English reader-translation, not an attested in-world sound-form.

`Proto-Balishan`, `Modern Balishan`, and similar labels date and locate a form. They do not by themselves
choose INH or SUB.

The protected personal-name set is deliberately phonologically diverse: **Xion** permits an initial written *x* and a
vowel sequence or palatal value; **Elara** permits an open trisyllable; **Valanar** permits repeated *a*
and intervocalic sonorants; **Kemvimore** permits four syllables and medial *mv* across a possible
morpheme boundary; **Rosik** permits final *-ik*. P0 also requires a transferable title and an institutional
phrase from the learned register. The future history must account for all seven outcomes, but it does not
have to force them through one naming register or one etymological layer.

## Superseded reconstruction notes

Everything below this heading records the first, corpus-accommodating reconstruction pass. It is retained
so its reasoning and coined forms are not mistaken for new discoveries later. It has **no current design
authority**. Statements that a test “passed,” that a route is “established,” or that a P1 form must cause
the language to change describe the retired method, not the present one.

## Findings from the superseded inventory pass

1. **None of the five protected names is presently impossible.** There is no established phonology for
   one of them to violate. They instead define phenomena the reconstruction must distribute across
   ordinary, dynastic, learned, and opaque house-name layers.
2. **Forms are firmer than meanings.** The existing guide assigns roots such as *el-* “star,” *bal-*
   “green,” and *lath-* “light/knowing,” but those are hypotheses awaiting sound history and corroborating
   vocabulary. They must not be used to generate more words yet.
3. **The apostrophe is not a family marker.** It occurs in locked or prose-established Halauri-looking
   forms and in an extensive but off-screen Terinoki/Bloodsworn corpus. The scripts may transliterate
   unrelated consonants with the same punctuation.
4. **Urathan map names are the clearest low-cost mismatch.** *Lumina Vale, Empirica, Memorium,* and
   *Agraria* are off-screen and currently pull toward the deliberately Latinate Erulian register. They
   should be tested first when Urathan phonology and scholarly word-formation are built.
5. **Silaris is available, not condemned.** With no prose or beat-draft footprint and no language family
   yet assigned, it can be renamed wholesale. Doing so before its people are linguistically designed
   would merely exchange one unsupported sound for another.
6. **Several future personal names can absorb later correction cheaply.** English- or modern-European-
   familiar forms such as *Emmett, Cordelia, Garrett,* and *Ashworth* deserve cultural review, while
   prose-established forms such as *Janice, Tobias,* and *Markus* should instead be explained through
   borrowing, translation convention, or the actual range of the language unless a serious conflict
   emerges.
7. **Personal names alone cannot choose the Balishan root split.** The corpus supports broad contrasts—
   royal open forms, harder common names, opaque house names—but regular vocabulary is required before
   INH and SUB assignments can be made responsibly.

## First ordinary lexical test — passed 2026-08-22

CON ***fen*** /fen/ “prepare plant remedies; herbal medicine as skilled work” survives unchanged through
Surface and Imperial Balishan because its stressed CVC form gives the sound history nothing to reduce.
Zero derivation supplies the activity, craft, and practitioner without an audible suffix. *Mira Fen* is a
personal name plus occupational byname; *Master Fen* is a translated honorific plus the trade used as a
complete working identity; *Xion Fen* is the deliberate entry of that common noun into an imperial
house-name field. None creates a surname or hereditary Fen family.

*Fen* is narrower than general “healer.” Master Keelen's apothecary proves that a practitioner may use a
personal name rather than carry *Fen* as his byname. The following medical-register test supplies the
broader terms without changing this boundary.

## First ordinary honorific test — passed 2026-08-22

CON ***sar*** /sar/ survives unchanged as an invariant preposed title for a socially established adult or
a person acting in a recognized skilled capacity. It is native *sar Fen, sar Janice, sar Keelen,* and *sar
Keya*; reader-facing English renders those as *Master Fen, Mistress Janice, Master Keelen,* and *Mistress
Keya*. Balishan itself marks no gender contrast.

Where the referent is unknown, reader-facing English may retain an established public form. Elara's “the
original Master Fen” occurs before Xion identifies his teacher and therefore does not imply a masculine
Balishan title or rename Mira as *Master Fen*.

The title sits outside the name and does not confer a house, profession, licence, or authority. Keya is
the controlling edge case: she is Xion's older patient, so the title cannot mean only “certified master of
a craft.” Noble *young master*, institutional headship, the Long Knives' *Mistress*, mastery grades, and
P3 *Master Garrett Caldris* remain separate English senses with uncoined native forms.

## First ordinary medical-register test — passed 2026-08-22

CON ***len*** /len/ zero-derives the broad practice and practitioner translated *healing/healer*. Learned
LOL ***sathen*** /ˈsa.θen/ < *sath-en* names a practiced diagnostic expert translated *physician*. The
categories overlap rather than rank: Xion is both, while Mira and Keelen are securely *len* without the
corpus requiring the learned label.

CON practical-site suffix ***-um*** /um/ regularly reduces to /əm/ in Surface Halauric. It produces
***lenum*** /ˈle.nəm/ “clinic” and ***fenum*** /ˈfe.nəm/ “apothecary shop/workroom.” These name continuing
organized practices, not bare rooms. English *apothecary* can consequently translate person *fen* or place
*fenum*; Balishan keeps them distinct.

The test does not coin *medicine, surgeon, midwife, bonesetter, hospital,* or foreign medical terms. It
also does not invent a licence: the palace physicians' robes mark institutional appointment, while Xion's
Book 3 self-description proves *sathen* itself is available on demonstrated knowledge and practice.

## First household and chartered-House test — passed 2026-08-22

CON ***dal*** /dal/ names the functioning household under one continuing provision, roof, and domestic
authority. It may include blood kin, married-in members, wards, servants, and dependants; it names neither
the building nor nobility. LOL legal ***sel-*** /sel/ “enter or constitute in authoritative public record”
precedes it in mixed EIB ***seldal*** /ˈsel.dal/, the chartered legal body translated as capitalized
***House***.

The corpus requires five independent axes: blood, House membership, second-name use, heirship, and present
office. Rosik marries into and later heads Kemvimore; Xion loses heirship before ceasing to bear its name;
he later enters Valanar by marriage. Marriage instruments therefore decide House entry for spouses and
children, while individual charters decide succession. No universal gender or birth-order rule is coined.

Fossil ***-annas*** /an.nas/ gives *Lar-annas*, “descendants or issue of Lar,” but is no longer productive.
House names otherwise share the legal slot rather than a suffix: native opaque *Kemvimore* and ***Hadris***
/ˈhad.ris/, neo-Halauri *Valanar*, foreign *Sa Ko Ren*, stolen *Dermir*, exceptional *Fen*, and
reader-translated *Greystone* all reach it by different routes.

***Hadris*** replaces P1 ***Valorian*** after a complete blast-radius check. *Valorian* falsely appeared
related to *Valanar* and worsened the series' already closed V-initial cluster; *Hadris* is compact,
native-compatible, collision-free, and changes no fact about Tobias or his House. Old editorial history
retains the former spelling where it records earlier work.
