# Draskin's Three Terms

**From before Book 1 through Book 8.** Leowin Draskin enters office through an ordinary election. He is a compromise candidate whom enough Provosts can accept, approximately five years into his first ten-year term when the series begins. His original accession has no exceptional mandate.

The Continental War changes his standing. He leads Uratha through the war during that first term, and when reelection comes due, fellow Provosts approach him and ask him to stand again on the strength of that service. The request gives him the customary social permission to seek a second term. His wartime reputation supports the candidacy; it does not alter the electorate or the counting rules. Only the seven sitting Tower Provosts vote. He wins the ordinary election and serves a second ten-year term.

For his third term, Draskin volunteers without being asked. A second term is unusual in Uratha; a third is almost unheard of. He accepts the social cost because he enjoys political work. He does not regard another decade in office as a sacrifice of the life he would rather be living.

He wins by making himself the least objectionable choice across competing Tower interests. He has enough initial support to survive the early eliminations, then gains transfers from Provosts whose preferred candidates have been eliminated and who rank him above the alternatives still standing. The victory uses the ranked election as written: he must eventually receive four votes, but need not begin as four Provosts' first choice. No exact ballot distribution is established. By Book 8, Draskin is serving his third consecutive term.
