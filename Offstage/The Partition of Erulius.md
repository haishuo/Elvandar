# The Partition of Erulius

**When:** After Kalden's death at the end of Book 7, before Book 8.
**Who:** Kalden's creditors, the twelve houses of the High Council and the Amber District banking houses;
Uratha; the Erulian Union; Gunastran.

## What the creditors held

Kalden mortgaged everything to fund his last war, and his creditors were his own: the twelve houses who had
counselled austerity and the banking houses of the Amber District. When he died, they owned the rump. That
much is recorded in `Book 7 - Last Debts/book7_outline.md`, *Who held the paper*.

**What they owned was not a country anyone could run.** It was sanctioned and landlocked, a third of it
already gone to the Union, with no prince, no army worth the name and no money. **They had never meant to
rule it.** They had bought it at a catastrophic discount and waited, and what they were waiting for was a
buyer. A state cannot be governed by nobody, and twelve houses who had spent twenty years being overruled
were not going to start governing one now.

## What they sold

**Gunastran bought futures on the mines.** The mines were still productive, and Gunastran is an industrial
nation. It shares no border with the rump and had no wish to govern Erulian hills. It wanted ore at a price
it knew in advance, and the creditors wanted money now.

**Uratha took the north**, including Eruliath and the Amber Palace. The border between them had always been
a soft administrative line, and the north was Uratha's natural share. **This was not Uratha gobbling up a
neighbour**, any more than Balisha gobbled up Silaris. A failed state on its doorstep had to belong to
somebody, and the creditors sold to the neighbour who could run it.

**The Union took the rest of the land, and did not want the capital.** It is a republic of freeholders that
seceded from Eruliath, and it remembers being taxed to death to pay for Kalden's court. Taking the Amber
Palace would have meant taking the old court, and the Union had already walked away from that once.

**The men who told Kalden not to do it were paid twice**: by Gunastran for the ore, and by Uratha for the
land. The Book 7 outline's verdict stands. In the ledgers of the people who funded him, he was a discount.

## What depends on it

**The map Book 8 happens on.** By the time Xion and Elara make their state visit, the Erulian Principality
no longer exists. Uratha's southern frontier runs through what was its north, and the Union holds the rest.
**Erulius still does not appear in Book 8**, as the Book 7 outline requires; the partition happens entirely
between the books.
