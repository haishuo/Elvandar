# The Vote on the Scholar's Crown

**Book 3, between the dispatches from Hauren and Draskin's departure for Kaha'an.** The Council voted on whether to offer permanent return of the Scholar's Crown to Balisha in exchange for expanded scholarly access to Hauren, exclusive to Uratha among foreign powers. The seven Tower Provosts and the Scholar-King divided **four to four**.

| For | Reason |
|---|---|
| Amethyst — Elaris Venn | The dispatches had persuaded Venn that her scholars needed sustained access to Hauren. She would administer the research herself. |
| Topaz — Tolrin Fax | Fax shared Emerald's respect for the trust Uratha had received. He also believed its engineers could learn to build and repair things whose workings had been lost. Putting that knowledge to use was part of what the academy owed the people it served. He wanted the chance to do the work himself. |
| Pearl — Petra Lind | Lind had to consider the patients her physicians still lost. She wanted Urathan physicians to be able to study whatever Hauren might teach them. |
| Ivory — Scholar-King Leowin Draskin | Draskin was prepared to return the Crown for an exclusive compact and to negotiate it with Elara in person. |

| Against | Reason |
|---|---|
| Emerald — Daven Harth | Harth still believed in the academy's founding purpose. In his judgment, accepting the Crown in trust placed an obligation on Uratha that endured even when it stood to gain as much as Hauren. |
| Onyx — Galvin Tarus | In the Ivory Tower, Uratha controlled who handled the Crown and who witnessed its silence. Transfer put both decisions in foreign hands. |
| Sapphire — Iyanna Mereth | Balisha had formally relinquished its claim that spring. Mereth opposed reopening a question Uratha had spent three centuries trying to settle. |
| Ruby — Kandor Belus | Belus knew how long the Crown had been silent. So long as Uratha held it, another sovereign could never test what military advantage its accumulated knowledge might confer. Balisha would keep both Hauren and the Crown if relations later soured. |

Draskin's casting vote carried the motion. **Exclusivity was the condition on which he could give up the Crown.** In Kaha'an he could accept one chamber being closed to Uratha. Letting Gunastran or any other foreign delegation in on the same terms would exceed his mandate. Fax in particular had staked his judgment on what the compact would secure for Urathan engineers. If Draskin surrendered the Crown for ordinary access, Fax would have grounds to withdraw his confidence. With five Provosts willing to remove him, Draskin could lose office. The four votes against the exchange committed their holders only on that motion.

Xion first learns of the offer at the negotiating table. He sees where Draskin gives ground and where he holds firm. The division in the Ivory Tower is known to Draskin as he negotiates and stays outside Xion's account.
