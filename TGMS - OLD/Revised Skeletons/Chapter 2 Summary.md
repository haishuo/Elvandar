### Chapter 2: Calling the Council

# Continuity Blurb for Chapter 2

- Xion urgently summons his reform group to The Amber Leaf teahouse—unprecedented behavior that immediately signals something serious has happened
- Silvanno Larannas (Water cartel heir) arrives first, noting how unusual it is for Xion to call meetings rather than being dragged away from his clinic
- When the full group assembles—Farleen Darfi (lesser Water family) and Kael Issamil (minor Iron clan)—Xion's usual reluctance has been replaced by urgent determination
- Xion reveals both Tam's capture by Slavers on fabricated debts and his sighting of a woman whose eyes changed color from blue to amber to violet
- The group reacts with unified skepticism due to frequent pretenders: Silvanno mentions the last "Princess Elara" had colored lenses, Kael suggests trauma-induced hallucination, Farleen pushes the "sophisticated pretender" theory
- Xion insists this was different—involuntary color changes with genuine emotions, not street performer tricks or manufactured props
- Despite their reasonable doubt, Xion remains adamant based on his medical training to trust clear observations: "I know what I saw"
- Farleen internally dreads where this revelation might lead her mission, walking a careful line between supportive friend and voice of reason
- The chapter establishes the context of frequent fake princesses while showing Xion's transformation from passive idealist to someone driven by impossible knowledge
- Ends with unresolved tension: Xion's unshakeable conviction versus his friends' unified skepticism, setting up the need for systematic investigation
