### Chapter 3: Process of Elimination

# Continuity Blurb for Chapter 3

- Xion approaches the mystery with the same methodical precision he brings to medical diagnosis, applying systematic elimination to investigate his sighting
- Despite their skepticism about yet another "Princess Elara," his friends reluctantly agree to help prove him wrong through thorough investigation
- Each participates for different reasons: Silvanno from loyalty despite doubt, Kael from desire to definitively disprove the claim, and Farleen from desperate hope that the search will fail
- Over several days, they systematically eliminate all possibilities: checking recent pretenders, Noble District households, middle district networks, merchant quarter contacts, and even safer Warrens areas
- Each dead end initially vindicates his friends' skepticism while making Farleen increasingly hopeful that Xion will abandon this dangerous path
- As the search proves fruitless, Silvanno and Kael begin worrying that Xion's obsession might be unhealthy grief over losing Tam, attempting gentle intervention
- Farleen becomes increasingly anxious about her next report to her handlers, as actively searching for a potential imperial heir represents concerning escalation from harmless charity work
- After exhausting every reasonable possibility including all known pretenders and likely hideouts, Xion reaches the inevitable conclusion: "The Arol Batae compound"
- His friends' concern escalates to genuine worry about his mental state, while Farleen faces the terrible realization she cannot talk him out of this course
- The systematic elimination transforms Xion's extraordinary claim from possible delusion into logical deduction, setting up the dangerous infiltration plan

18. They suggest his trauma over losing a patient might be manifesting as elaborate fantasy
19. **Farleen's Mounting Anxiety**: She becomes increasingly anxious about her next report to her handlers
20. She's been able to characterize their activities as harmless charity work that poses no threat to cartel stability
21. But actively searching for a potential imperial heir—even one they believe is fake—represents concerning escalation
22. Her arguments become more frantic as she tries to convince Xion to abandon the search
23. **The Inevitable Conclusion**: After exhausting every reasonable possibility, Xion reaches the logical endpoint
24. "We've checked everywhere else. There's only one place left where someone with the real royal trait could be hidden."
25. When pressed by his increasingly worried friends: "The Arol Batae compound. They were the imperial guard."
26. "If Princess Elara actually survived The Rending, if she's been protected all these years, that's the only place that makes sense."
27. **Everyone's Escalating Concern**:
    - Silvanno: "Xion, that's insane! Even if you're right—which you're not—that place is impenetrable."
    - Kael: "You're talking about infiltrating the most secure compound in the city based on what's probably a hallucination."
28. **Farleen's Desperation**: "Please, Xion, just let this go. These old guards chose isolation for a reason. What would even be the point of such a dangerous gamble?"
29. Her arguments become more frantic as she realizes she'll have to report that Xion intends to breach the most secure location in Kaha'an
30. She knows what orders that revelation will likely bring from her handlers
31. **Xion's Unshakeable Logic**: "When you've eliminated everything else, whatever remains must be the answer, no matter how improbable or dangerous."
32. His friends are now genuinely worried about his mental state and judgment
33. **Farleen's Terrible Realization**: She cannot talk him out of this course that will force her to choose between the man she loves and the mission she's been given
34. **Chapter Conclusion**: The systematic search has transformed Xion's claim from possible delusion into logical deduction
35. The stage is set for the dangerous infiltration that will force all hidden loyalties into the open
36. Farleen must now prepare to make an impossible choice between love and duty
