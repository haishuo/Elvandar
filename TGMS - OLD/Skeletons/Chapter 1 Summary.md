# Continuity Blurb for Chapter 1

- Xion Kemvimore, heir to the Grain cartel, secretly operates as "Master Fen," a healer in Kaha'an's poorer districts
- Xion treats a boy named Tam with a severe hand injury, reflecting on the irony that his father controls food while children scavenge for scraps
- Xion meets with his reform group: Silvanno Larannas (Water cartel heir), Farleen Darfi (lesser Water family), and Kael Issamil (minor Iron clan)
- The group has been doing small charitable acts but feels frustrated by their limited impact
- Farleen reports that the Slavers cartel is now collecting debts for other cartels, turning minor infractions into opportunities for enslavement
- Xion reveals rumors that an imperial heir may have survived The Rending and is being protected by the Arol Batae (former imperial guard)
- The group believes a legitimate heir could challenge the cartel system with claims of imperial legitimacy
- They decide to seek information from a broker named Arlo Berakailen
- City guards interrupt their meeting but are deterred when they recognize Silvanno and Xion as cartel heirs
- Romantic tension exists between Xion and Farleen, culminating in a brief kiss as they part
- Xion views Kaha'an's problems through a healer's perspective - treating symptoms vs. curing the disease
