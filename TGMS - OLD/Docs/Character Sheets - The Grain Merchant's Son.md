# Character Sheets – The Grain Merchant’s Son

This file provides structured reference entries for all major characters in *The Grain Merchant’s Son*. It is used to guide voice, internal monologue, behavior, power dynamics, and relational tone. These entries are LLM-optimized and designed for cross-chapter consistency.

---

### Protagonists

---

## 👤 Xion Kemvimore  
**Role:** Protagonist; heir to House Kemvimore and the Grain Cartel  
**Faction:** House Kemvimore (Grain)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Early twenties  

**Appearance:**  
- Slender but no longer frail  
- Dark hair worn longer than fashionable  
- Features favor his mother; expressive eyes that betray emotion  

**Voice & Personality:**  
- Quiet, methodical, emotionally sincere  
- Compassionate and idealistic, yet burdened by guilt  
- Suppresses anger and fear under a shell of calm analysis  
- Struggles between moral clarity and pragmatic survival  

**Background:**  
- Born before The Rending; mother died in the catastrophe  
- Frequently ill as a child—learned healing from a household herbalist who was later executed by Rosik  
- Raised under the austere discipline of his father  
- Holds quiet reverence for those who show mercy in an unforgiving world  

**Skills:**  
- Talented herbalist and battlefield healer  
- Observant reader of social cues and power dynamics  
- Trained in basic self-defense, but not a seasoned fighter  

**Motivation:**  
- Seeks to ease suffering and restore legitimacy to Kaha'an  
- Believes a just ruler—not cartel tyranny—could break the city's vicious cycle  

**Special Items:**  
- A medallion bearing the Long Knives symbol, given to him by Rosik—both a burden and a cipher  

[See full profile: `Xion Kemvimore.md`]

## 👤 Princess Elara Valanar  
**Role:** Hidden heir to the imperial throne of Kaha'an  
**Faction:** Unaligned; trained by the Arol Batae  
**Gender:** Female  
**Age:** Around 20 (was an infant during The Rending)  

**Appearance:**  
- Athletic build from years of training  
- Moves with the unconscious precision of a born commander  
- Eyes change color based on emotion (a royal trait):  
  - Blue (calm), Amber (anger), Silver (fear), Emerald (joy), Gray (sorrow), Violet (determination)

**Voice & Personality:**  
- Direct, strategic, physically imposing  
- Possesses natural charisma but little familiarity with political subtleties  
- Expresses herself through action rather than rhetoric  
- Driven and controlled—her emotional discipline is legendary  

**Background:**  
- Raised in isolation by the Arol Batae after The Rending  
- Trained in martial and strategic disciplines, not courtly traditions  
- The world believes she died—but she has survived in the shadow of myth  

**Skills:**  
- Elite-level hand-to-hand and armed combat  
- Battle-tested tactical and logistical insight  
- Incredible emotional self-control  

**Motivation:**  
- Initially survival and identity  
- Later, reclaiming her imperial legacy and reforming a corrupted Kaha'an  

**Special Connection:**  
- Unexplained intuitive pull toward Lathion and the Brass Door—possible link to ancient technology or prophecy  

[See full profile: `Elara Valanar.md`]

### Antagonists

---

## 👤 Rosik Kemvimore  
**Role:** Lord of the Grain; cartel patriarch and antagonist  
**Faction:** House Kemvimore (Grain)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Early fifties  

**Appearance:**  
- Tall, lean, with prematurely silver hair  
- Aristocratic bearing, cultivated and deliberate in every movement  

**Voice & Personality:**  
- Cold, calculating, and utterly pragmatic  
- Uses silence as both weapon and wall  
- Believes that only ruthless order can prevent societal collapse  
- Seldom shows emotion; when he does, it is either rehearsed or involuntary  

**Background:**  
- Former assassin with ties to the Long Knives guild  
- Son of "The Mistress," current head of the guild  
- Married into House Kemvimore and assumed control after his wife's death during The Rending  

**Skills:**  
- Master manipulator and strategist  
- Deep knowledge of economic, social, and violent control mechanisms  
- Can weaponize both charisma and intimidation with equal ease  

**Motivation:**  
- Believes a strong, centralized authority—preferably under his own hand—is essential for Elvandar’s stability  
- Willing to destroy a thousand lives if it prevents a million from starving  

**Current Goals:**  
- Seeks control of the Ruby Throne, but not hastily—he watches Elara’s emergence with tactical detachment  
- May either seek to destroy her, co-opt her, or use her as a pawn

## 👤 Farleen Darfi  
**Role:** Noble from a minor Water cartel house; opportunist with shifting allegiances  
**Faction:** House Darfi (Water-aligned)  
**Gender:** Female  
**Age:** Early twenties  

**Appearance:**  
- Poised and stylish, always dressed to impress in middle-tier nobility fashion  
- Amber eyes—identical in shade to The Mistress, a fact noted with discomfort by some  

**Voice & Personality:**  
- Initially warm and cooperative, later reveals herself as adaptive, strategic, and self-interested  
- Speaks in careful, polished tones, with veiled barbs beneath diplomatic phrasing  
- Quick to read shifting power dynamics and pivot accordingly  

**Background:**  
- Entered Xion’s reform circle as a seemingly sincere supporter  
- Betrayed Xion during a covert meeting by stabbing him and fleeing the scene  
- Known to have communicated with both Rosik and The Mistress; motives remain unclear  

**Skills:**  
- Social maneuvering, coded speech, and reading political undercurrents  
- Proficient in knife use and short-range weapons—trained, likely by the Long Knives  
- Possesses courtly grace and ruthless pragmatism beneath the surface  

**Motivation:**  
- Desires personal advancement and long-term survival in cartel-ruled Kaha’an  
- Believes loyalty is a liability for the powerless  

**Current Goals:**  
- Operating independently in the Warrens to establish new “connections”  
- May be positioning herself as a potential middle-tier power broker

## 👤 Ronas Dermir  
**Role:** Warlord of the Slavers cartel; power broker in the Warrens  
**Faction:** Slavers Cartel  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Late 30s to early 40s  

**Appearance:**  
- Towering, broad-shouldered, covered in ritual scars and brutal marks of survival  
- Deliberately unkempt and intimidating—projects brutality at a glance  

**Voice & Personality:**  
- Outwardly vulgar, crass, and loud—a persona tailored to rule by fear  
- In reality, highly pragmatic, observant, and calculating beneath the thuggish veneer  
- Speaks with a deep growl, but often laces his speech with surprising insight  

**Background:**  
- Born in the Warrens with no house or heritage  
- Fought his way up through pit-fights, local militias, and assassinations to claim control  
- Built the Slavers cartel through intimidation, structure, and brutal but effective order  

**Skills:**  
- Intimidation, tactical brutality, and wartime logistics  
- Knows how to control unstable lieutenants with fear and incentives  
- Negotiates through pressure, not pretense  

**Motivation:**  
- Views himself as the last bulwark between controlled cruelty and chaotic collapse  
- Resents the “civilized” cartels for sneering at his methods while using his services  
- Sees himself as a necessary evil in a rotting system  

**Current Goals:**  
- Maintain control of the Warrens and suppress internal coups  
- Deter outside factions from attempting to eliminate or replace him  
- Leverage his indispensability to gain reluctant respect from the upper cartels
