# Timeline – *The Grain Merchant’s Son*

## Metadata

- **Document Type**: Narrative Timeline  
- **Scope**: Major events of *The Grain Merchant’s Son*  
- **Coverage**: Begins Spring 1044 BR  
- **Chronology**: Linear, time-stamped progression  
- **Date Format**: `Day X, Month Y, Year Z BR`  
- **Calendar System**: Balishan Reckoning (BR) — 10 months/year, 36 days/month, 6-day weeks  
- **Main Characters Featured**: Xion Kemvimore, Elara Valanar, Silvanno Larannas, Rosik Kemvimore  

---

## Act I – The Healer and the Hidden Girl

### 📅 Day 15, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 1 – The Healer and the Heir**  
- Xion Kemvimore, heir to the Grain cartel, operates in secret as "Master Fen," treating the poor in Kaha'an.  
- He treats a boy named Tam and reflects bitterly on the disparity between his father's control of food and the suffering he witnesses.  
- That evening, Xion meets with his reform group—Silvanno Larannas, Farleen Darfi, and Kael Issamil—discussing increased brutality from the Slavers cartel.  
- Rumors surface that a surviving imperial heir is being protected by the Arol Batae.  
- As they part ways, romantic tension between Xion and Farleen is implied.

---

### 📅 Day 16, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 2 – Secrets and Suspicions**  
- Rosik Kemvimore reveals that he knows of Xion’s reformist activities and summons him to a rare gathering of all four cartel leaders.  
- At the gathering, Xion learns how to contact the elusive broker Arlo Berakailen, and resolves to fund the investigation using most of his inheritance.  
- Sa Ko Ren offers cryptic remarks about Xion’s late mother; Melina warns her brother that he’s under scrutiny.  
- At dawn, Xion and Farleen meet a representative of Berakailen who demands he return alone at midnight with triple payment.

---

### 📅 Day 17, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 3 – The Truth at a Price**  
- Xion meets with Arlo Berakailen and learns the imperial heir is Princess Elara Valanar, whose color-changing eyes mark her as a Valanar.  
- He spends nearly his entire inheritance to obtain this truth.  
- Multiple people—including Melina, Kael, and Rosik—warn Xion not to trust those closest to him.  
- Sa Ko Ren confronts him directly about his reform group.  
- Rosik surprisingly assists Xion by giving him detailed logistical data on Arol Batae compound deliveries.  
- Xion becomes increasingly suspicious of Farleen.

---

### 📅 Days 18–20, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 4 – Betrayal’s Edge**  
- Xion gives each member of his reform group false delivery information to test loyalty.  
- Silvanno passes the test; Farleen fails, appearing at the correct location despite false details.  
- Xion studies the Arol Batae compound and prepares to infiltrate it during a grain shipment.  
- Rosik gives him a medallion bearing an unfamiliar symbol that predates House Kemvimore’s ascendancy.

---

### 📅 Day 21, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 4 (cont.) – The Infiltration**  
- Xion enters the Arol Batae compound disguised as a grain hauler.  
- He sees Elara and witnesses her eyes shift to violet, confirming her lineage.  
- Farleen confronts Xion inside the compound, confesses her betrayal, and attacks him with a dagger.  
- Elara unexpectedly intervenes and saves him.

---

## Act II – Sanctuary, Secrets, and the Warrens

### 📅 Day 22, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 5 – Behind the Walls**  
- Xion awakens in the Arol Batae compound with his wounds being treated.  
- The medallion is identified as a Long Knives emblem, raising alarm among the Inner Circle.  
- Farleen spreads misinformation, claiming Xion was killed by Arol Batae security.  
- She secretly meets her father to report Xion’s presence.  
- Elara admits to having followed Xion on previous occasions and expresses curiosity about him.  
- After much debate, the Arol Batae decide to let Xion stay temporarily.  
- A quiet bond begins to form between Xion and Elara, rooted in shared burdens.

---

### 📅 Day 25, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 6 – Expanding Alliances**  
- Three days later, Xion and Elara’s relationship deepens through discussion and shared perspective.  
- Elara opens up about her isolation and her lack of knowledge about the world outside.  
- The Mistress of the Long Knives is revealed to be Rosik’s mother.  
- She confers privately with Rosik about Elara, referencing ancient "arrangements."  
- Silvanno and Kael’s investigation draws cartel attention.  
- Elara proposes bringing them into the compound for a controlled introduction, challenging Commander Vesk’s authority.

---

### 📅 Day 27, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 7 – A Princess Decides**  
- The Inner Circle approves Elara’s plan to bring Silvanno and Kael into the compound—her first major decision overriding Commander Vesk.  
- Vesk reports increased Long Knives activity, implying dormant interest has reawakened.  
- Tania Larannas quietly allows her son’s investigation to continue, moved by memory of his father’s idealism.  
- The Mistress meets with Farleen, reaffirming that events are following long-standing “arrangements,” and instructs her to prepare for Xion’s return.

---

### 📅 Day 29, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 8 – Beyond the Compound**  
- Mira observes Elara in the eastern courtyard, allowing Xion to signal Silvanno and Kael.  
- Elara meets with both men, confirming her identity through her color-shifting eyes.  
- The group discusses transforming governance, with Elara noting that any new order must *include*, not destroy, existing power blocs.  
- Farleen remains in the shadows, monitoring and feeding information to her father.

---

### 📅 Day 31, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 9 – Into the City**  
- Elara insists on seeing Kaha’an firsthand and prepares for a cloaked reconnaissance mission.  
- During the outing, Water and Iron cartel forces unexpectedly converge on their observation post.  
- In the escape, Keiran is captured defending the group; Elara, Xion, and Farleen flee through collapsing tunnels toward the Warrens.

---

## Act III – The Princess Ascends

---

### 📅 Days 31–32, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 10 – The Depths Below**  
- The group traverses old distribution tunnels and contacts Smoke of the Chimney, who offers neutral passage.  
- Farleen shows her own Long Knives medallion, then leaves to pursue “connections.”  
- Elara feels a powerful connection to an ancient brass door, which glows blue when she touches it.  
- Fleeing Long Knives assassins, they unknowingly cross into Slavers territory.

---

### 📅 Day 32, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 11 – The Warrens’ Embrace**  
- Xion and Elara disguise themselves in local garb.  
- An elderly storyteller references the royal eye trait; a blue headwrap woman gives Elara a bone token for safe passage.  
- Elara feels a strange pull while crossing a deep chasm.  
- A Slavers patrol captures them, recognizing the Long Knives medallion as cause for detainment.

---

### 📅 Day 33, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 12 – The Slaver’s Court**  
- Ronas Dermir receives the pair and displays surprising nuance and restraint.  
- A rogue captain attacks the brass door and is killed by Ronas to preserve the truce with the Long Knives.  
- Ronas grants Xion and Elara safe passage to meet The Mistress, also tasking Xion with informing Rosik of grain shortages to the Warrens.

---

### 📅 Day 34, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 13 – The Mother of Blades**  
- Two Long Knives assassins escort Xion and Elara to a hidden sanctuary.  
- The Mistress cryptically references pre-Rending “arrangements” and obligations.  
- She speaks of Xion’s mother’s “conviction,” suggesting deep familiarity, and implies her own connection without naming it.  
- Elara is examined but not opposed. The Mistress warns that the cartels are mobilizing.

---

### 📅 Day 35, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 14 – A Throne Decided**  
- Commander Vesk confirms Keiran is being interrogated about a “noble woman.”  
- Elara, visibly affected, declares her intention to openly claim the Ruby Throne.  
- The Inner Circle plans to approach Sa Ko Ren first for support.  
- Rumors of “the girl with royal eyes” begin to spread through Kaha’an.

---

### 📅 Day 36, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 15 – Preparations for Power**  
- The Arol Batae begins strategic mobilization.  
- Rosik instructs Melina to publicly support Elara, subtly manipulating the political narrative from behind the scenes.  
- House Benizar offers support—hedging with other factions simultaneously.  
- Sa Ko Ren receives word of Elara’s intent and prepares to receive her, cautiously intrigued.  
- Citywide cartel security presence increases, confirming the secret is out.

---

### 📅 Day 37, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 16 – Trial by Combat**  
- Sa Ko Ren challenges Elara to a duel, stating, “If you seek to rule, prove you can fight for it.”  
- The duel ends in a draw, followed by formal recognition of Elara’s claim—marking the first true fracture in cartel unity.  
- Iron districts begin flying the imperial banner; other factions react with wary positioning.  
- Farleen rescues the injured Keiran from cartel custody. He reveals interrogation focused on **tunnels beneath the Ruby Palace**.

---

### 📅 Day 38, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 17 – The Ruby Throne**  
- Elara leads a growing procession toward the Ruby Palace.  
- Tania Larannas halts them, offering Water cartel recognition in exchange for maritime control.  
- Inside the palace, Elara navigates halls she’s never seen but somehow remembers.  
- Upon touching the imperial seal, ward lights activate—confirming her bloodline.  
- Palace guards kneel; horns ring out—Elara has reclaimed the Ruby Throne.

---

### 📅 Day 41, Month 3, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 18 – The New Order**  
- Three days into her reign, district reactions vary: Iron celebrates, Grain and Water watch cautiously, Slavers resist.  
- Ronas Dermir arrives with Slaver enforcers, reminding Elara of his control over the Warrens.  
- Merchant caravans begin spreading word of her ascension across Elvandar.  
- A traveler named **Varen** arrives at the eastern gate bearing a sealed letter from the **Erulian Principality**.

---

### 📅 Day 12, Month 4, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 19 – Remembrance Day**  
- On the Rending’s anniversary, Elara makes her first public appearance.  
- The ceremony becomes a spontaneous coronation as crowds acclaim her Empress.  
- Prince Kalden of Erulia confirms her existence and deploys his **Shadow Corps**.  
- Lady Orisa Benizar is exposed as a traitor, secretly aligned with Erulian agents.  
- A coordinated infiltration strikes the palace—**Farleen saves Elara**, while **Keiran is mortally wounded**, fulfilling his oath.

---

### 📅 Day 15, Month 5, Year 1044 BR  
**Chapter 20 – One Month Later**  
- One month into her reign, Elara’s court becomes the diplomatic center of Kaha’an.  
- All seven successor states send messages—Erulia’s message thinly veiled as a threat.  
- Investigations begin into the **brass door** and **subterranean systems**, guided by Elara’s intuition and Keiran’s final intelligence.  
- Prince Kalden prepares full military escalation.  
- Farleen, still recovering, requests to speak with Xion privately—Elara allows it, uneasily.  
- The chapter ends with Elara on the Ruby Throne—**secure for now, but surrounded by forces already moving against her**.
